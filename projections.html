Why there is no 2030–2035 number

The calibration problem

What a model would need

Executed rent through the verified term, unreimbursed expenses, debt, renewal options, the legal obligor, a market cap rate, insurance terms and owner tax rates. None of these are public records held here.

Compounding a 2020–2025 population change forward to 2035 would assume the driver persists — something no official release asserts. The result would be an artefact of the rule.

CA DOF estimates ↗

What is defensible instead

Dated observations, official long-range regional projections with their stated uncertainty, published statutory rates, and scenario stress tests that need no invented probabilities.

A future implementation would still require private documents, model design, testing and a documented after-tax comparison before it could be relied upon. This is a research framework, not a calibrated or back-tested forecasting system.

SCAG adopted forecast ↗

Official long-range context

Planning forecasts, not property forecasts

SCAG's adopted Connect SoCal 2024 forecast projects six-county employment from 8,937 thousand in 2022 to 10,276 thousand in 2050 (+0.50% per year) and accommodation and food service jobs from 832 thousand to 938 thousand (+12.8%). It also states that the region's population has declined since a January 2019 peak. These are statutory planning projections with explicit uncertainty; they are not a central-LA parcel forecast and are not mixed with Census V2025 estimates.
