Jack in the Box Inc. SEC Audit, NNN Mechanics, Prop 13, Measure ULA & IRC §1031

Primary SEC Form 10-K/10-Q/IR analysis of Jack in the Box Inc. (CIK 0000807882), real-estate ownership split, and California/LA City property & transfer tax rules.

Tenant & Tax Takeaway: Verify Corporate vs. Franchisee Signature, Guard the 35-Yr Prop 13 Rule & Use §1031

Jack in the Box's system contracted from 2,191 (FY24) to 2,115 units (Q3 FY26) with −1.1% Q3 comps and 7.624% June 2026 secured debt refinancing. Critically, FY2025 10-K Item 2 reveals 1,585 units are leased by Corporate from third-party landlords vs. 387 franchisee-direct units — making lease-obligor verification Gate #1. On exit, IRC §1031 shields ~27%+ Fed/CA gain taxes, while City of LA sales must respect the July 1, 2026 Measure ULA $5,400,000 (4%) and $10,900,000 (5.5%) cliffs.
