What official geography can say about a Central LA site &mdash; and what it cannot

Three official geographies, a verified demand base, and seven site attributes that require the address.

Central Los Angeles is not an official statistical geography

The brief says 'around central Los Angeles'. No Census, BLS or City of Los Angeles statistical product is published under that name. Rather than invent a boundary, every figure in this file is labelled with one of three official geographies, and never blended.

Official geography Official definitionWhat it is used for here Why not the parcel
Los Angeles CountyCensus county, FIPS 06037Population, income, housing, hazards, property taxToo broad to describe a trade area; used for direction only
Los Angeles cityIncorporated placeCity minimum wage, Measure ULA, transfer taxRegulatory boundary, not a demand boundary
Los Angeles-Long Beach-Santa Ana MSABLS metropolitan areaEmployment, unemployment, labour forceLabour market, not a retail trade area
Parcel / trade areaAddress-specific—Withheld: identifying the parcel breaches the privacy directive
