Rules this file follows

  • Official government or statutory sources first; SEC filings for anything about the tenant.
  • A private research firm is never presented as an official source, and its figures are never labelled verified.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed on an official page, it is left blank and flagged. It is never inferred, interpolated or carried over from an aggregator.
  • Every row states its own geography and period; rows from different vintages are not blended.
  • Analysis is labelled analysis, including the entire model.

Not an appraisal, legal opinion, tax return or personalised investment advice. Analysis is conditional on an anonymised subject and on evidence published up to 2026-11-07. Missing values are withheld, never estimated.

Every table row links to its publisher. Verify status is defined on Method & privacy. Flagged items: Flagged for review.
