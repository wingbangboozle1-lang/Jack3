Read the unemployment rate with the labour force

4.4% in September 2026 looks healthy. The labour force fell from 6,768,502 to 6,646.9 thousand over the year — roughly 121,600 fewer people. A rate that falls because people leave the workforce is not an expanding labour market, and the two numbers are published together here so the favourable one cannot be read alone.
