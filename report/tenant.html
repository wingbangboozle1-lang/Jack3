The decisive quotation

The issuer states that the restaurants targeted by its closure programme are largely ones in the system for over three decades, and that remaining closures occur based upon respective franchise agreement termination dates. A lease expiring between 2030 and 2035 is exactly that kind of date. This does not mean the site will close. It means the owner should not assume renewal, and should establish the site's status before committing capital to either a renewal negotiation or a marketing process.

Source: Jack in the Box Inc., Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended 2025-09-28; Jack in the Box Inc., Q4 and full-year 2025 earnings release (Exhibit 99.1).

Why this is credit evidence, not restaurant criticism

In a single-tenant NNN structure the owner's return is not a function of property management — there is nothing to manage — but entirely of whether one counterparty keeps wanting the site. Three things therefore matter: the tenant's ability to pay (corporate guaranty), its willingness to stay (store-level economics), and its legal option to leave or stay (the option clause). The first is unknown, the second is disclosed as deteriorating, and the third is disclosed as the tenant's choice.

What would upgrade the tenant assessment

  • Written confirmation that this site is outside the closure programme.
  • A corporate guaranty that survives franchisee failure.
  • Site-level sales and rent-coverage data, which only the tenant or the owner can supply.
  • A current credit rating read directly from the agency — deliberately withheld here (F-06).
