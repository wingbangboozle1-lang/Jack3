The decision

A projection you can audit, not a number you have to trust

Three coherent scenarios, a sourced discount anchor, every assumption exposed, and the worst case reported alongside the expected case.

Why this model is in units of R

The rent was never supplied, so no dollar figure could be used without inventing one. Every output is therefore expressed in R = one year of the current contractual base rent. Sell-versus-hold compares two streams from the same asset, so the rent level largely cancels out of the ranking, which is what the decision needs. Multiply by your own rent for dollars.

Method

  1. Three coherent scenarios. Each assumption carries a favourability direction. The optimistic case takes the favourable extreme of every assumption; the pessimistic case the unfavourable extreme of every one. Without that rule a sensitivity grid silently mixes optimism and pessimism.
  2. Official anchor, declared premium. The discount rate is the verified U.S. Treasury 10-year par yield of 4.19% at 2026-01-02 plus a risk premium swept from 2.0% to 5.5%. The premium is labelled judgement; the anchor is sourced.
  3. Risk adjustment. Transaction friction, vacancy downtime, re-tenanting capital, a tenant-survival probability and a catastrophe expectation are applied inside each strategy, not bolted on afterwards.
  4. Reported by worst case as well as expected value. The owner does not get to choose which scenario arrives.

What the model cannot do

It cannot price this parcel. It has no trade-area data, no rent, no basis and no zoning. It reports rankings under stated assumptions, and those rankings are stable across all three scenarios for the sell-versus-renew choice, which is the choice the owner actually faces. It is not a forecast, and no probability is attached to any specific outcome for this site.

Not an appraisal, legal opinion, tax return or personalised investment advice. Analysis is conditional on an anonymised subject and on evidence published up to 2026-11-07. Missing values are withheld, never estimated.
