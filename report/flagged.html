Known limitations of this file

  • The subject property is unidentified, so every site-specific conclusion is conditional (R-14).
  • Vintages are mixed by necessity and stated per row (F-14).
  • Cap rates and comparables are not officially published (F-02, X-09).
  • All model probabilities and risk scores are judgement, swept across ranges (F-15).
  • Source pages can change after the access date; the snapshot is dated and is not a live feed.

Not an appraisal, legal opinion, tax return or personalised investment advice. Analysis is conditional on an anonymised subject and on evidence published up to 2026-11-07. Missing values are withheld, never estimated.
