What can go wrong, how likely it is judged to be, and what to do about it

Fifteen business-as-usual risks and seven black-swan events, scored, evidenced and paired with a mitigation.

Likelihood and impact are judgement, not data

The score is likelihood × impact on a 1-5 scale, and it is our analytical judgement. It is shown so the reasoning can be challenged. Each row cites the verified evidence it rests on, and that evidence is official or issuer-published.

Black-swan events

These are low-probability, high-impact events that could destroy local economic activity. They are not hypotheticals imported from elsewhere: one of them has already happened inside the analysis window.

ID Event and mitigationScoreEvidence base Source
B-01Wildfire
A wind-driven wildfire destroys the trade area. The January 2025 Eaton and Palisades fires burned 14,021 and 23,448 acres and destroyed 9,414 and 6,837 structures, removing an estimated $5.2-10.1 billion of output.
Mitigation: Confirm the tenant maintains full replacement-cost insurance and that the owner is named; check the parcel's Fire Hazard Severity Zone.
2×5=10County of Los Angeles fact sheet; CRS IF12871County of LA, wildfire fact sheet ↗
B-02Earthquake
A major earthquake. USGS puts 30-year probabilities for the Los Angeles region at 60% (M6.7+), 46% (M7.0+) and 31% (M7.5+).
Mitigation: Verify seismic insurance and deductibles; a long hold adds exposure that no rent escalation compensates.
3×5=15USGS UCERF3; ShakeOut scenarioUSGS, earthquake probabilities ↗
B-03Tenant failure
A brand-level failure or accelerated closure wave; the system has already shrunk from 2,191 units (FY2024) to 2,136 (FY2025) and 2,115 (Q3 FY2026).
Mitigation: Corporate guaranty, security deposit, and early-warning lease covenants.
2×5=1010-K FY2025; Q4 FY2025 release; Q3 FY2026 10-QJack in the Box Inc., Q4 FY2025 release ↗
B-04Flood / debris flow
Post-fire debris flow or riverine flooding; insurance availability and cost change sharply after a wildfire season.
Mitigation: Check FEMA flood zone and the post-fire moratorium status before renewal.
2×4=8CRS IF12871 (post-fire insurance moratorium)CRS In Focus IF12871 ↗
B-05Extreme heat
Sustained extreme heat suppresses drive-thru and dine-in traffic and raises refrigeration energy cost.
Mitigation: Diversify tenant mix; ensure HVAC and refrigeration capital obligations are assigned to the tenant.
3×3=9Seasonal consumption pattern (analysis)BLS, LA CPI ↗
B-06Out-migration
Out-migration accelerates beyond official projections, compounding the measured -3.2% county decline.
Mitigation: Monitor annual Census estimates; set a review trigger at a further 1% decline.
2×4=8Census V2025; CA DOFCensus QuickFacts, LA County ↗
B-07Insurance
Insurer withdrawal from California commercial property makes coverage unavailable or unaffordable, transferring the risk to the owner under NNN.
Mitigation: Require proof of insurance annually; negotiate a lease covenant that makes non-renewal a default.
