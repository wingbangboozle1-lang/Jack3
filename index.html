WEALTH PRESERVATION VS. GROWTH

Why timing before 2029 matters—and when holding still wins.

Wealth-preservation floor: For a low-basis owner prioritizing capital safety, a pre-expiry IRC §1031 exchange in 2027–2029 (or an early extension followed by §1031) avoids facing a dark box, CapEx, or franchisee non-renewal into JIB’s August 2029 $432.25M debt wall—provided gross pricing stays clear of City of LA ULA dead zones ($5.40M–$5.63M and $10.90M–$11.07M).

When holding or repositioning wins: If the executed lease runs to 2035 with a corporate master guaranty, store sales clear JIB’s $1,874k brand AUV, and ZIMAS shows C2/MIIP/CHIP or Metro D Line transit density, residual land value can justify holding—so long as an extension does not cross Prop 13’s 35-year reassessment line.

Five unverified private gates that control the call

No site-specific valuation or categorical sell/hold verdict is fabricated without these owner documents:

1. Executed lease & options: exact expiry (2030 vs. 2035), notice dates, NNN scope, and remaining years vs. 35-year Prop 13 rule.
2. Legal obligor & unit P&L: JIB Inc. master lease vs. single-purpose franchisee LLC, store sales, and rent coverage.
3. Jurisdiction & zoning: City of LA vs. adjacent city, Measure ULA applicability, Ord. 183893 seismic status, and CHIP/transit tier.
4. Broker pricing & §1031 basis: unlevered BOV relative to 4.86% UST spreads, adjusted basis, and §1250 recapture.
5. Parcel hazard & insurance checks: CGS EQ Zapp, FEMA FIRM, FHSZ map, and 2028 commercial coverage bindability.
