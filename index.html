One property. One decision.
One reconciled file.

A Jack in the Box triple-net property in Central Los Angeles, primary term expiring 2030–2035. Six parallel research layers were merged, every decisive figure was re-read on its official publisher, and the conflicts between the layers were resolved in the open.

RECOMMENDATION

Sell before expiry — extend first only if the tenant commits in writing.

Selling now has the highest worst-case present value of every strategy tested (11.62 R, against 1.18 R for renewing to a 2030 expiry). Renewing with a contracting tenant ranks last in both the base and the pessimistic scenarios. If, and only if, the tenant signs a long extension now, extend and then sell the lengthened lease.
