CONDITIONAL RECOMMENDATION · ANALYST JUDGEMENT, NOT A VALUATION

Run both tracks now: market the asset for sale, and demand renewal terms in writing.

Start a confidential sale process 24–36 months before the earliest expiry, in parallel with a written renewal request. Convert to whichever result the documents support: a verified after-tax sale, or an enforceable extension of ten years or more at market rent with acceptable credit. What is not defensible is letting a 2030–2035 option clock run out on regional averages alone.

WHY NOW RATHER THAN AT EXPIRY

Every verified trend is flat or negative.

City population is 0.8% below its 2020 estimates base and the county is 3.2% below. Metro payrolls grew 0.5% year over year, led by education and health while information fell 5.7%. The tenant reports system same-store sales of −1.1% and is closing 150–200 restaurants, with the remainder tied to franchise-agreement termination dates.

An expiring lease transfers negotiating leverage to the party that can wait. Marketing while income is contractual, and while the tenant still needs the site, is the lower-risk way to preserve capital.

What would change this view

A signed extension at market rent backed by acceptable credit; a verified after-tax bid above the hold case; block-level evidence that this trade area is growing; or a change in the owner's liquidity needs.

None of these can be established from the regional data published here. The property's rent, basis, debt and lease are not public records we hold.

Risk balance

Business as usual, then the tail

Business as usual

Income concentration. One tenant, one building, one lease. A vacancy costs the full rent, plus taxes, insurance and re-letting.

Wage and cost pressure. Covered fast-food work pays $20.00/hour under AB 1228, and the state floor is $16.90/hour from 2026. Rent coverage at the store level is unknown.

Rates and exit yields. The 10-year Treasury was 4.84% on 5 November 2026. A higher exit yield lowers price for the same rent.

Schools. Los Angeles Unified graduates 86.5% of its four-year cohort (2025 Dashboard), a district-wide outcome that says nothing about the campus assigned to any one site.

Taxes. A city sale above $5.4m carries a 4% Measure ULA surcharge on top of the 0.45% city base tax; the county charges $0.55 per $500 and bills the full $1.10 per $1,000 inside Los Angeles; §1031 defers, and does not eliminate, capital-gain tax.

Tail events that can end the local economy

Wildfire. In 2025 the Palisades and Eaton fires destroyed 16,264 structures in this county in 24 days. Insurance availability and pricing can change for the whole region afterwards.

Earthquake. USGS models a plausible M7.8 southern San Andreas event at ~1,800 deaths and ~$213bn in losses. Parcel-level ground-failure exposure has not been checked.

Flood, and correlated utility or pandemic disruption. The FEMA map service is the official source; no parcel zone has been assigned.

Tenant failure or accelerated closure. The issuer's own programme closes underperforming units and sells owned real estate. Neither establishes this site's fate, but both remove the assumption that a chain lease is permanent.
