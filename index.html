SP-01 — Decision file

Should the owner sell before the lease expires, or renew? A documented answer from official sources, with every figure traceable and every gap disclosed.

Recommendation

Renew if the tenant will commit. Never sell for cash. Route any exit through a §1031 exchange.

On risk-adjusted after-tax wealth the ranking is stable across 6,000 simulated macro paths: renew-and-hold-with-a-deferred-exit first, a §1031 exchange executed now second, and an outright cash sale last. The outright cash sale is dominated in every scenario tested, and the reason is tax, not the market.

What the research found

1

The tenant is stabilising, but it is also shrinking — on purpose F-01

Jack in the Box same-store sales went from −7.4% in Q4 fiscal 2025 to −1.1% in Q3 fiscal 2026, a 6.0 point improvement (S12, S13). That is the strongest single positive datapoint in this study. Against it, the company has guided to closing 50 to 100 restaurants in fiscal 2026 after closing 86 in fiscal 2025, and its system is down to 2,115 units from 2,191 (S12, S13). The tenant is recovering its P&L by removing its weakest boxes. Whether SP-01 is one of them is not publicly knowable.

2

The local economy is stable but not growing, and the population is shrinking

Los Angeles County unemployment fell to 4.8%, below both California (5.1%) and its own 5.6% a year earlier (S05). But total nonfarm employment grew only 0.4% over the year, and five of eleven industry sectors are shrinking — information lost 10,200 jobs and has declined in 20 of the last 22 months (S05). The county has lost 3.2% of its population since 2020 (S06) and is projected to lose 240,800 public school students over the next decade, the largest county decline in the state (S09). Health care is the only real growth engine.

3

Density is the asset. Central LA holds 3.4× the county's population density

The City of Los Angeles carries 8,304 people per square mile against 2,467 for the county (S06, S07), and the city is declining more slowly than the county (−0.8% versus −3.2% since 2020). Its population is younger (14.2% aged 65+ versus 17.1%) and the restaurant sub-sector is still adding jobs (+7,300 year over year, S05). An urban infill drive-through is therefore in the better half of a deteriorating county.

4

The tax code, not the market, decides this question

A cash sale in the City of Los Angeles carries 0.56% in county and city transfer tax below the Measure ULA threshold, and 4.56% above $5.4 million or 6.06% above $10.9 million because ULA is a cliff applied to the whole price (S16, S17). Stack a blended 33.4% capital gains rate on the gain and an outright sale surrenders roughly a third of all accumulated appreciation. A §1031 exchange defers that entire amount (S18). Stack that on selling costs and the arithmetic is brutal: on a $6.5 million asset with a 40% basis, a cash sale delivers $4.83 million of net proceeds — a 25.7% haircut taken before a dollar is reinvested. The same-day §1031 exchange of the identical asset delivers $5.94 million. Nothing about the property changed between those two figures; only the structure did.

5

A short lease is priced. That is why waiting is not free

Once a single-tenant net-lease asset has under about five years of term left, buyers stop pricing a bond and start pricing a re-leasing story. The model applies a cap-rate penalty that widens to 75bp as remaining term goes to zero (A16). This is the mechanism by which 'hold to expiry' loses to 'sell before expiry' even in a rising-rent market — and it is the single reason a renewal is worth more than the rent it captures.

6

The hazard record is recent, large, and already inside the tax base

In one week of January 2025, two urban wildfires destroyed 16,263 structures in Los Angeles County — 6,845 in the Palisades Fire and 9,418 in the Eaton Fire — with 29 confirmed civilian deaths between them (S21, S22). The fiscal consequence is visible: wildfire assessment relief removed more than $9 billion from the 2026 assessment roll, more than new construction added (S25). A central-LA infill site is far less exposed than the wildland interface, but the event class is not hypothetical.

7

The tenant's cost of capital has more than doubled, and it matures before the lease does

Jack in the Box refinanced in June 2026 by issuing $500 million of 7.624% secured notes to retire paper carrying 4.476% and 3.445% (S14). Its next anticipated repayment date is 2029 — earlier than the 2030–2035 expiry window. The tenant therefore faces a refinancing decision before the landlord faces a renewal decision, and a tenant under refinancing pressure is a tenant that renegotiates rent.
