EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Income durability first.
Growth second.

The regional evidence is mixed: employment is growing modestly, population is below its 2020 estimates base, and the chain reports weaker same-store sales. These indicators justify diligence, not a categorical sell decision.

For wealth preservation, assess enforceable rent, tenant credit, owner liquidity, after-tax alternatives and uninsured downside together. An extension may protect income; a sale may reduce concentration. Neither is demonstrably superior with the current evidence.
