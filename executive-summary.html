Executive Summary

Whether to sell The Property (a Central Los Angeles quick-service restaurant on a NNN lease to a Jack in the Box operator) before the lease expires in 2030–2035, or to renew. Findings, a recommendation, the price of each path, and what must be verified before money moves.

1 · The Question Decision framing

One asset, two mutually exclusive endgames inside the 2030–2035 expiry window: (A) Sell during the income-producing window — while the NNN rent is contractual and the cap rate can be underwritten off it — or (B) Renew/extend with The Tenant and hold through the next lease term. Every factor that moves value was examined: geography & hazards, income levels, population trajectory, school systems, employment & job growth, transfer-tax friction, tenant credit, interest rates, and black-swan scenarios.

3 · Recommendation What to do, in order

Renew with pre-committed sell triggers — executed early, not at the deadline.
  1. 2026 Q4–2027 Q1: Re-underwrite the unit (12 months of sales, rent coverage, closure-program status). Commission 2 broker opinions of value and a seismic/FHSZ parcel report.
  2. By 2027-06-30: Deliver a renewal term sheet: 10-year extension, ≥2.0% annual escalation (CPI floor 2%, cap 3.5%), 2×5-year options, corporate or unit-level guarantee, renewal signed ≥24 months pre-expiry.
  3. Standing trigger: If no signed extension by 24 months pre-expiry, OR unit sales decline >15% YoY, OR Tenant enters JIB closure program, OR escalation offered <1.5% — list for sale immediately (income stream intact) targeting close ≥12 months pre-expiry.
  4. If sale is triggered: price just at/under the ULA cliff only if the market supports it legitimately (pricing below $5.3M solely to dodge ULA with a $5.4M asset is not honest pricing — get real comps); model 1031; net proceeds after ULA/DTT/commissions/cap gains must beat the risk-adjusted hold value.
  5. Never hold into vacancy. If the lease expires unsigned, convert immediately: re-tenant, sell as pad, or pursue ground lease / redevelopment — decided by 30 days post-expiry, not “wait and see.”

Why not “sell now”? Selling today forfeits the income stream that justifies the price, crystallizes ULA + cap-gains friction into a soft cap-rate market, and resets the buyer’s Prop 13 basis upward (making future NNN buyers demand a higher cap). Why not “renew blindly”? Because tenant credit (F3) and submarket income (F1) argue for contractual protections, not a hoped-for renewal at the 11th hour. Balance = early renewal with hard sell triggers.

The address, APN, lease, rent roll, escalations, option language, environmental reports, and The Tenant’s store-level P&L were not provided and cannot be researched publicly with reliability. Every such item is on Flagged for Review. Action on any trigger above requires those documents first.
