BASE CASE · CONDITIONAL RECOMMENDATION

Prepare both tracks; do not choose sale or renewal on regional averages alone.

Privately verify the lease and notice dates now, explore sale interest and written renewal terms, and compare after-tax outcomes, downside risks and owner needs. Risk balance for wealth preservation

Manageable risk, not zero risk

Concentration can be a major risk

A single-tenant net lease looks safe until the term ends. Holding to expiry concentrates tenant credit, lease-rollover, rate and catastrophe risk into one short window. Selling early spreads the decision across several years and converts an illiquid single asset into a choice.

The cost of acting early is real: transfer taxes, a possible 4% ULA surcharge, capital-gain tax and reinvestment risk. Those are computable from published rules once the owner's figures exist.

Why not simply renew

Assess renewal term, rent, concessions and obligor credit together against sale and re-letting alternatives. The issuer's chain-wide results cannot establish this lease or tenant. An extension may change sale flexibility and bids; neither effect is guaranteed.

Black-swan exposure is not diversified by renewing: the same parcel keeps the same earthquake, wildfire and flood exposure. Insurance verification and a concentration cap on net worth are required under either route.
