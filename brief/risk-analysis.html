Black-swan doctrine: you cannot buy your way out of a black swan after it starts. For fire/earthquake/unrest the only real mitigants are bought before: admitted insurance with ordinance-or-law coverage, seismic retrofit, and a lease that places repair/rebuild obligations on the Tenant to the maximum lawful extent. Get those three in place this quarter, not at renewal.
