Reading the Outlook Synthesis

Net read: flat-to-soft county economy through 2027 (unemployment 5.9–6.1%), carried by healthcare and government; DTLA corridor policy targets a step-change in population/jobs by 2040; rates are off the 2021 lows; cap rates are at 12-year highs. That is not the backdrop for an opportunistic “sell into strength.” It is the backdrop for securing a contractual NNN income stream early and exiting only on a trigger. The 2030–2035 expiry window sits far enough out that a signed 10-year extension now (2026–2027) covers most of it — the decision is being made on the Tenant’s credit today, not on 2030 resale conditions.
