3 · Methodology

  1. NOI projection: rent compounds at escalation e per year: NOIₜ = rent × (1+e)ᵗ. NNN means no OpEx deduction (tenant pays taxes/insurance/maintenance).
  2. Exit valuation: price = NOIₜ ÷ cap rate. Cap rates are mark-to-market assumptions, anchored to 2026-11-04: STNL mean 6.5% (M&M via CRE Daily, industry), JIB-quoted NNN 5.0–6.0% (broker, industry) — never treated as official.
  3. ULA: applied to FULL consideration when price crosses tier (2025-07-01 tiers; annually CPI-indexed each July 1).
  4. Total hold-then-sell = Σ rent over hold + (price − ULA). Excludes cap gains/recapture/commissions — see friction table.

4 · Limitations — Read This

  • The $275,000 rent and 2.0% escalation are illustrative placeholders, not the actual lease. Actual values must come from the lease/estoppel.
  • Scenario math is not an appraisal (USPAP) and not a CMA. It encodes no location premium/discount beyond your own cap-rate input.
  • ULA applies to City of LA transfers only. If any portion of consideration qualifies for exemption, the Office of Finance decides — not this model.
  • Cap-rate inputs are industry quotes, flagged Industry; they are stale within weeks. Re-cut the grid at listing time with two live broker opinions.
  • All figures as of 2026-11-06. Nothing here is legal, tax, or investment advice.
