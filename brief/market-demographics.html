Boundary & Method Note Read before relying

  • “Central LA” is defined differently by each dataset: Point2Homes’ Central LA neighborhood composite (832,615 residents), Census PUMAs (e.g., Central/Koreatown PUMA 79500US0603733), and City Planning’s Central City Community Plan area are NOT the same geography. Figures above are labeled with their own geography and are not mutually interchangeable.
  • Census values reached in this environment came from reputable third-party mirrors labeled Mirror; the primary source is census.gov QuickFacts/ACS. Re-pull before any transaction decision.
  • ACS 5-year estimates carry sampling error (e.g., ±$392 on the county median). Small-area (neighborhood) estimates have wider margins.
  • No personal information is included; the asset is identified only as “The Property”.
