Triple-Net Lease Expiring 2030–2035

A decision analysis for the owner of a quick-service Jack in the Box property in Central Los Angeles on a NNN lease: whether to sell before expiry or renew at expiry, grounded in verified official data on demographics, employment, schools, hazards, taxes and tenant credit — with an explicit risk register and a projection model.

Headline RecommendationBalanced risk & growth

Recommended course of action — “Renew with triggers, don’t wait to be forced to sell.”
Open renewal / extension negotiations with The Tenant now (2026) — from a position of strength while the rent is being paid — targeting a 10-year extension with ≥ 2.0% annual escalations (or better: CPI-linked), signed no later than 24 months before expiry. Pre-authorize a sell trigger: if no signed extension exists 24 months pre-expiry, or The Tenant’s unit enters the closure program, or coverage deteriorates, list the property 18–24 months pre-expiry while NNN rent is still in place. Do not hold into vacancy: a dark QSR without an income stream trades far below its NNN value, and selling post-expiry would compound Measure ULA / reassessment costs against a lower price.
