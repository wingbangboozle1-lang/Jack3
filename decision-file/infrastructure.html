What can be said without the address

Two observations are supportable from verified regional data and do not depend on the parcel.

First, Los Angeles County accommodation and food services sales were $44,016,586,000 in 2022, and the City of Los Angeles accounted for $17,366,966,000 of that — 39.5% of the county total in a city with 39.8% of the county population. The demand pool is large and proportionate. The tenant is not in a market that lacks spending; it is in a market where its own transaction counts are falling, which points to brand and site-level problems rather than to an absence of demand. Source: U.S. Census Bureau 2022 Economic Census via QuickFacts.

Second, the county has 437,788 employer establishments and 1,184,030 in the City of Los Angeles, with annual payroll of $550.7 billion countywide. Employer-establishment density of 172.8 per square mile in the county and 464.6 in the city indicates a genuinely dense urban commercial environment. That supports alternative uses for the land — which is the single most important fact for strategy S6, and it is verified. Source: U.S. Census Bureau County Business Patterns and QuickFacts.

The infrastructure conclusion

Whatever the specific transit project, the verified evidence is that this is a dense, high-employment, high-spending urban market whose population is shrinking while its commercial base remains very large. For a landlord that combination favours the land over the building: the improvement is a specialised 1,372 square foot drive-thru box, but the site sits in one of the densest employer markets in the United States. That is why the model gives strategy S6 (hold then redevelop) real weight — and why the zoning lookup in the Input Register is worth paying for before dismissing it.
