Geography and Market Definition

“Central Los Angeles” is not a legal or statistical geography. This page records exactly what it was mapped to, why, and what that means for every figure on the site.

R-5 — the market label is not an official geography

“Central Los Angeles” has no FIPS code, no Census definition and no statistical boundary. It is used variously to mean the Central City planning area, the Wilshire corridor, a wider central subregion, or simply “not the Valley and not the beach”. Every aggregate statistic quoted on this site therefore belongs to one of three official geographies, and each is labelled.

What this mapping costs the analysis

County and metropolitan aggregates are the wrong granularity for a single drive-thru restaurant. A quick-service site trades on a drive-time ring of a few minutes, and Los Angeles County contains 2,492 census tracts with more than ten million dollars of building value each — with radically different demographic profiles. A county population decline of 3.2% is entirely compatible with a specific trade area growing.

The honest position, and the one taken here, is that these aggregates establish direction and context, not site-level demand. They are sufficient to establish that the owner cannot assume demographic growth, and that the tenant's reported transaction declines are consistent with the regional picture. They are not sufficient to establish that this particular corner is deteriorating. That requires the address.

What becomes possible once the address is supplied

Census tract population, households, median income and vehicle availability from ACS table B03002 and related tables; CAL FIRE Fire Hazard Severity Zone from the 2025 map; FEMA flood zone and National Risk Index tract score; City of Los Angeles zoning and Community Plan Area; Caltrans traffic census at the nearest station; assigned schools and their California School Dashboard ratings; LA County assessed value and tax rate. Every one of these has an official source, and every one is listed on the Flagged for Review page with its lookup route.
