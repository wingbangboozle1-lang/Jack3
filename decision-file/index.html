Sell - and if the tenant will extend first, extend, then sell.

The model gives selling now the highest worst-case present value of every strategy tested (11.62 R against 4.82 R for the highest-expected-value alternative). Renewing the Jack in the Box lease ranks last in both the base and the pessimistic scenario, because it is the only strategy whose entire value depends on a counterparty that is publicly contracting and that has tied its closure decisions to exactly the dates in question. Eight findings that drive the recommendation

FindingEvidenceStatusOfficial source
The tenant is contracting, and its closure trigger is the lease expiry date.Jack in the Box Inc. discloses a 150–200 restaurant block closure programme. Remaining closures occur “based upon respective franchise agreement termination dates”. The system fell from 2,191 restaurants at FY2024 year end to 2,115 at 2026-07-05.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
The county is losing population, and the official forecasts disagree on the sign.Census Vintage 2025 measures −3.2% since the 2020 estimates base. SCAG’s 2020 plan projected +16.6% to 2045; the state projection has the county falling to about 9.3 million by 2040. This analysis underwrites to the measured trend, not to either forecast.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
The labour market looks better than it is.Unemployment fell from 5.5% to 4.4% year on year, but the civilian labour force also fell by about 121,600 over the same period. A falling rate driven by people leaving the labour force is not an expanding economy.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
The tenant’s sector is one of the few growing ones.Leisure and hospitality added 11.5 thousand jobs (+1.5%) in the Los Angeles metro area in the year to September 2026, while information fell 5.7% and manufacturing 1.2%. Total nonfarm rose only 0.5%.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
A statutory wage ratchet compresses the tenant’s margin.California’s $20.00 fast-food floor applies to franchisee employers and cannot be avoided by restructuring. A council exists with authority to raise it further through 2029.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
The tail risk is already realised in this county.The January 2025 Eaton and Palisades Fires destroyed 16,251 structures and cost $5.2–10.1 billion in economic output. USGS puts the 30-year probability of an M6.7+ earthquake in the region at 60%.VERIFIEDOfficial source ↗
