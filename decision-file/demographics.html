The finding that matters most

Los Angeles County has lost population in every year since the 2020 estimates base, at an official −3.2% over five years. A drive-thru quick-service restaurant sells to people inside a short drive-time ring. A shrinking base erodes transaction counts — which is exactly what the tenant reports. Any hold strategy that depends on demographic growth is underwriting against the measured trend.
