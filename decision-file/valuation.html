The one number that decides it

Selling now has the highest worst-case present value of any strategy. In the pessimistic scenario it returns 11.62 R, against 9.88 R for the next-best strategy and 1.18 R for renewing the Jack in the Box lease to a 2030 expiry. Under the stated objective — preservation of wealth at a manageable level of risk — the floor matters more than the ceiling, because the owner cannot choose the scenario in advance.

How much of the answer depends on the cap rate

Between a 5.0% and an 8.5% cap rate the same income stream is worth 20.0 R or 11.8 R — a 41% swing in value from an input nobody can officially verify. That is why the recommendation is framed around the worst case rather than the expected case: selling now wins the pessimistic scenario outright, and it does so at every cap rate in the band, because it also removes the cap-rate risk itself.

Breakeven — what would have to be true to hold instead of sell

Breakeven questions

QuestionAnswer from the model
At what exit cap rate does holding to expiry beat selling now?Not at any rate in the tested band on a worst-case basis. Holding wins the optimistic scenario only, where the exit cap rate falls to about 5.0% AND the alternative use of the land is worth 20 R.
At what probability of tenant non-renewal does renewing stop being viable?Renewing already ranks last at the base assumption of 45% non-renewal. It only competes if non-renewal probability falls below roughly 25% AND downtime below 0.5 years AND the replacement rent at or above current rent.
How long can the site stand empty before re-letting destroys the hold case?In the pessimistic case, about 3 years of downtime plus 3.0 R of re-tenanting capital takes a re-let strategy from 12.30 R to 2.51 R. Vacancy, not the exit cap rate, is the fastest way to lose the hold case.
Does the length of the remaining term help?Yes for holding, but weakly. Collecting rent to 2035 rather than 2030 adds about 1.3 R of discounted rent, while adding three more years of catastrophe and tenant-closure exposure. The model does not reward waiting.
