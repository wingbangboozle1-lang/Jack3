Everything on this page is taken from filings the tenant made with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, or from the tenant's own earnings release filed with the SEC the same day. SEC EDGAR CIK 0000807882. No broker commentary, no press summary, no inferred figure.

Why the tenant dominates this decision

In a single-tenant NNN structure the property's income is one credit. The tenant's own filings describe a 150–200 restaurant closure programme in which the remaining closures occur “based upon respective franchise agreement termination dates”. A lease expiring between 2030 and 2035 is that date. This is the single most important fact in the entire file.
