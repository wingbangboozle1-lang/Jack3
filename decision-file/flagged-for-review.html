F-02 — Capitalisation rate and comparable sale prices for single-tenant NNN restaurant property HIGH NO OFFICIAL SOURCE

What the issue is: No United States government agency publishes commercial capitalisation rates, commercial sale comparables, or single-tenant net-lease pricing. This data is private and sold by commercial vendors.

Why it is blank: Values are therefore NOT asserted anywhere on this site. Valuation is presented as a sensitivity grid across an explicit cap-rate range, and every cap rate in the model is labelled ASSUMPTION - NO OFFICIAL SOURCE.

How to resolve it: Commission an MAI appraisal and obtain current transaction comparables from a licensed commercial broker. Bring both to the model as the cap-rate input.

F-03 — FEMA National Risk Index values for Los Angeles County HIGH UNVERIFIED

Why it is blank: Left blank rather than repeated from a secondary source. The official FEMA tool is linked so the value can be confirmed for the specific census tract.

How to resolve it: Open FEMA's Resilience Analysis and Planning Tool and query the subject tract. Tract-level wildfire loss in Los Angeles County is extremely concentrated, so a county average is not a usable proxy.

Other irregularities found during the analysis

Los Angeles city race and ethnicity shares do not reconcile. The QuickFacts race-category shares returned for Los Angeles city sum to roughly 74%, while the equivalent county shares sum to 99.8%. One or more city values appear to have been mis-extracted during retrieval. The city race breakdown is omitted from the Demographics page entirely rather than published with a possibly wrong figure (F-12). None of the decision-relevant variables — population, income, poverty, density, foreign-born share, age structure — is affected.

Los Angeles city building permits and housing units show [X]. The Census Bureau marks these as not available at place level. They are rendered blank with the QuickFacts note reproduced, and county values are never substituted (F-13).

Two official forecasters disagree on the sign of population change. This is not an error on this site — it is a genuine conflict between SCAG and the California Department of Finance, published side by side on the Official Forecasts page and resolved by underwriting to measured Census data instead.

SEC EDGAR search ignores date filters. The EDGAR browse interface returned the most recent filings regardless of the requested date range. Filing history was therefore assembled from the filing index pages directly. Recorded so that any reviewer reproducing the work does not assume a date-filtered query worked.
