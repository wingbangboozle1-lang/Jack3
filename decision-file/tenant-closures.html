What this means for PROPERTY-01

Three disclosures combine into one exposure. First, the programme targets restaurants “in the system for over three decades”. Second, the remaining closures happen “based upon respective franchise agreement termination dates”. Third, the company has said the programme is running slower than planned because franchisees must evaluate lease obligations before closing.

A long-operating site with a lease expiring in 2030–2035 matches the stated profile on all three counts. That does not mean it will close. It means the owner should not assume renewal, and should establish the site's status with the tenant before committing capital to either a renewal negotiation or a marketing process.

The asymmetry the owner faces

Under the disclosed structure, renewal options are generally exercisable at the company's sole discretion. If the owner's lease follows the same pattern, the option to extend belongs to the tenant, not the owner. The owner is therefore long an asset whose continuation depends on a decision made by a counterparty that is publicly contracting. Confirm from the lease document whether the options run in the tenant's favour or the landlord's — this single clause changes which strategies are even available.
