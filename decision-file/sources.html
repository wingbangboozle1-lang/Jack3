Sources deliberately excluded

The following categories were consulted during research and then rejected. None appears in the register above, and no value from any of them is asserted anywhere on this site.

  • Commercial net-lease listing and aggregator sites. They publish Jack in the Box cap rates, prices and franchisee names. Used only to establish that no official cap-rate source exists. Rejected as evidence, and rejected as a privacy risk.
  • Media restatements of government data. Where the government page was reachable, the government page was used. Where it was not, the value is blank.
  • Analyst ratings and price targets. Private commercial opinion.
  • Rating-agency credit opinions. Private issuers, not official sources. The tenant credit page therefore publishes no rating.
  • Investor-forum and social commentary. Not evidence of anything.
