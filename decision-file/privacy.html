Privacy and Anonymisation

Personal information privacy is the highest-priority constraint on this project. This page records exactly what was found, what was removed, and how the guarantee is enforced mechanically rather than by promise.

Guarantee

No owner name, entity name, street address, Assessor Parcel Number, telephone number, email address, transaction price, rent figure, purchase basis or any other personally or property-identifying value appears anywhere in the published site, in the data snapshot, or in the repository deliverables. Where such a value would be needed for the analysis, the field is left blank and labelled INPUT REQUIRED or replaced with the placeholder PROPERTY-01 / OWNER-01.

What was found and how it was handled

Mechanical enforcement

The guarantee is enforced by code, not by care. tools/pii_scan.py runs over every generated file in docs/ and over every data module, and fails on:

  • email addresses and telephone-number patterns;
  • street-address patterns (a number followed by a street-type word);
  • Assessor Parcel Number patterns;
  • the words “APN”, “owner name”, “mailing address” in a value position;
  • any personal name appearing in a data value rather than in prose about a public corporation.

tools/check_links.py separately confirms that every URL rendered into the site is well formed and that no URL from a rejected non-official domain appears in a column that claims to be verified.

Internal-only material

The repository's .gitignore excludes scratch/, private/ and pii/ so that any working material containing identifying detail can never be committed or published by accident. Those directories do not exist in this repository and were never populated, because no identifying material was ever supplied.

Site indexing

Every page carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">. The site is intended for the owner and their advisers, not for public discovery.
