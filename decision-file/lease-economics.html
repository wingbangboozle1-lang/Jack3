NNN Lease Economics

How a triple-net single-tenant restaurant lease actually works, what the tenant's own disclosed lease terms reveal about its behaviour, and where the owner's exposure sits.

What NNN means here

The structural insight

NNN leasing is often described as passive ownership. It is not. It transfers every operating cost to the tenant and leaves the owner with 100% of the continuation risk. The owner's return is therefore not a function of how well the property is managed — there is nothing to manage — but entirely of whether one counterparty keeps wanting the site. Every page of this file is about that single question.

What the tenant's own leases reveal

The owner's lease document was not supplied. The best available evidence of how this brand behaves at expiry is the lease terms the issuer itself discloses in its Form 10-K — as both lessee of hundreds of restaurants and lessor to hundreds of franchisees.
