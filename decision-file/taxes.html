F-05 — no tax rate is published here

The relevant official pages were not successfully retrieved during this analysis: one Internal Revenue Service URL and one California Franchise Tax Board URL both returned HTTP 404. Third-party sources state a California top rate of 13.3% and a 3 1/3% real-estate withholding, but none of these figures was read on an official page, so none is asserted.

Publishing an unverified tax rate on a site the owner will rely on for a seven-figure decision is precisely the failure mode this project was told to avoid. The mechanisms below are described structurally and linked to their official sources. Get a written opinion from a California-licensed CPA before acting on any of it.

How tax interacts with the decision

Three points are structural and do not depend on any rate.

  1. Net proceeds decide the comparison, not gross price. The model applies friction before comparing strategies. A 4.5% friction assumption is a placeholder; the real figure includes tax, and tax on a long-held depreciated building can be very large.
  2. A like-kind exchange is not automatically the right answer here. It defers tax, but it requires buying more real estate. If the reason to sell is concentration in one illiquid single-tenant asset in one shrinking county, an exchange preserves exactly the exposure the sale was meant to remove. This trade-off should be made deliberately, not by default.
  3. Holding is not tax-free either. A hold keeps collecting taxable rent, keeps the building depreciating toward a larger recapture position, and defers the decision into a period when the tenant's own closure programme is actively evaluating the site. The tax cost of waiting is real even though no tax is payable while waiting.
Action

Item 5 of the sequenced action plan on the Decision Framework page: obtain a written California tax opinion covering gain, depreciation recapture, section 1031 eligibility, withholding and any deferred-gain reporting obligation, before signing a listing agreement. Bring the official source links in the table above to that conversation.
