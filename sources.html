Sources & methodology

Evidence first. Interpretation second. Unknowns stay unknown.

Verification standard

Verified rows are source-matched observations re-read from the publisher page on 10 November 2026; the current audit record is in research/VERIFICATION-2026-11-10.md in the repository. Department of Education district data for Los Angeles Unified was also read; no school attendance boundary can be assigned to an unidentified parcel. A link is not a guarantee that a rolling source will retain an earlier value. Verified means the source reports the observation—not that it predicts this property. Issuer reports are labeled separately from government data.

Analysis rows are our conditional reasoning. Their official links support background context only; they do not endorse the recommendation. Unverified rows contain no invented value; links are research routes or explicitly limited context, not proof of missing facts.

QuickFacts observations use different vintages, geography and survey periods. ACS estimates have sampling error; no tract estimates or margins of error were obtained. The BLS November 4, 2026 summary uses unadjusted data and may be revised. Hazard context is drawn from USGS scenario modelling and CAL FIRE incident records, not from parcel-level analysis. No model has been trained or back-tested; no causal claim about property value is made.

Privacy and publication boundary

Asset A and Owner A are placeholders. No addresses, parcel IDs, owner names, contact details, financial accounts or private lease records are collected or published. No analytics, third-party embeds or external scripts are used. GitHub hosting may retain standard access logs. Public source links open their publishers’ websites.

Reproducibility and maintenance

Public evidence is stored in data/evidence.json; scripts/build.py generates every page. scripts/validate.py checks table provenance, local links and blank-value status. Source values were reviewed manually; automated validation does not assert live external availability or semantic verification. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live data feed. Refresh official releases and reassess geographic fit before acting.

Earlier companion reports, including the /advisory/ layer and its numerical projections, are retained in the repository but excluded from this public site pending independent validation; see Flagged for Review. No hidden property records or private evidence were inferred. Missing private documents cannot be supplied through autonomous public research.
