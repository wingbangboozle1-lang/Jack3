Sources & methodology

Every numerical fact on this site traces directly to an inspected primary government, municipal, court/tax, or SEC/issuer filing reviewed on 8 November 2026 (UTC). Unverified private property facts remain blank.

Verification & evidence discipline

Primary-source rule: All demographic, wage, assessment-roll, transfer-tax, property-tax, yield-curve, school, planning, transit, and catastrophe metrics are read directly from their issuing U.S. federal, State of California, Los Angeles County, or City of Los Angeles agencies (including direct binary parsing of CA Dept. of Finance P2A_County_Total.xlsx and DIR News Release 2026-66 resolving prior item F-17). Corporate credit and real-estate footprint figures come from Jack in the Box Inc.’s FY2025 Form 10-K, Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q, and official investor releases, labeled separately as Issuer records.

Zero fabricated probabilities or cap rates: Companion-layer rankings that relied on unverified scenario weights (20/50/20/10%), assumed cap rates, or assumed personal tax brackets remain excluded from GitHub Pages. Instead, model/area_projection.py computes pure statutory Measure ULA arithmetic and exact algebraic 1.00 R break-even thresholds anchored to the November 6, 2026 10-Year U.S. Treasury yield (4.86%).

Vintage & geographic boundaries: QuickFacts mixes V2025 population estimates with 2020–2024 ACS 5-year estimates and 2022–2023 economic surveys; BLS September 2026 figures are preliminary and not seasonally adjusted; LA County Anniversary Fact Sheet wildfire counts (6,837 / 9,414) and CAL FIRE 2026 updated incident counts (6,845 / 9,419) are both preserved transparently. Regional averages never override parcel-level underwriting.

Privacy, reproducibility & Pages curation

No owner names, parcel IDs, APNs, street addresses, account details, third-party analytics, or external scripts are collected or published. data/evidence.json and scripts/build.py deterministically build these nine static HTML pages; scripts/validate.py, scripts/stage_pages.py, tools/pii_scan.py, and Jekyll CI verify byte-identical curation and official source links on every table row.
