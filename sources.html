Sources & methodology

Evidence first. Interpretation second. Unknowns stay unknown.

Verification standard

Verified rows are source-matched observations from a prior review on 6 November 2026; key Census, BLS, issuer and wage rows were rechecked on 7 November. The CDE school dashboard page was also retrieved on 7 November; the relevant school remains unknown. A link is not a guarantee that a rolling source will retain an earlier value. Verified means the source reports the observation—not that it predicts this property. Issuer reports are labeled separately from government data.

Analysis rows are our conditional reasoning. Their official links support background context only; they do not endorse the recommendation. Unverified rows contain no invented value; links are research routes or explicitly limited context, not proof of missing facts.

QuickFacts observations use different vintages, geography and survey periods. ACS estimates have sampling error; no tract estimates or margins of error were obtained. The BLS November 4, 2026 summary uses unadjusted data and may be revised. No model has been trained or back-tested; no causal claim about property value is made.

Privacy and publication boundary

Asset A and Owner A are placeholders. No addresses, parcel IDs, owner names, contact details, financial accounts or private lease records are collected or published. No analytics, third-party embeds or external scripts are used. GitHub hosting may retain standard access logs. Public source links open their publishers’ websites.

Reproducibility and maintenance

Public evidence is stored in data/evidence.json; scripts/build.py generates every page. scripts/validate.py checks table provenance, local links and blank-value status. Source values were reviewed manually; automated validation does not assert live external availability or semantic verification. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live data feed. Refresh official releases and reassess geographic fit before acting.

Earlier companion reports are retained in the repository but excluded from this public site pending re-verification; see Flagged for Review. No hidden property records or private evidence were inferred. Missing private documents cannot be supplied through autonomous public research.
