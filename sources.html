Verification standard

What 'Verified' means here

Verified means the publisher page was re-read on 2026-11-11 and states that observation, with the geography, period, unit and vintage shown in the row. It does not mean the observation predicts this property. Derived means arithmetic on two published observations; the arithmetic is printed in the row. Analysis is our conditional reasoning and its link supplies background context only. Unverified rows have an empty value cell and an entry on the Flagged for Review page.

QuickFacts observations use different vintages, geographies and survey periods. ACS estimates have sampling error; no tract estimates or margins of error were obtained. The BLS summary updated 2026-11-04 uses unadjusted data and may be revised. Hazard context comes from USGS scenario modelling, CAL FIRE incident records and FEMA programmes, not from parcel-level analysis. No model was trained, fitted or back-tested.

Privacy and publication boundary

Highest-priority constraint

Asset A and Owner A are placeholders. No address, parcel ID, owner name, account handle, contact detail, financial account, tax basis, debt statement or private lease record is collected or published. Official filings and agency pages encountered during research contain named contacts, emails, telephone numbers and street addresses; none of that is reproduced here. The site has no forms, analytics, tracking, third-party embeds or external scripts. Public source links open their publishers' websites; GitHub hosting may retain standard access logs.

Reproducibility

One dataset, one generator

The evidence register lives in data/canonical_rows.py and is exported to data/canonical.json. scripts/build_canonical.py renders every page; scripts/validate.py checks row provenance, blank values, official hosts, internal links and personal information; scripts/stage_pages.py builds the exact Pages artifact and compares it with Jekyll's output. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live data feed.
