California State Board of Equalization
California Property Tax: An Overview (Publication 29)
https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/pub29.pdf
as of 2023 edition
What this source verifies (5)
  • Proposition 13 limits the property tax rate to 1% of assessed value plus incremental rates for locally voter-approved bonded indebtedness
  • Reappraisal at current fair market value occurs only on a change in ownership or completion of new construction (base year value)
  • Annual increases in the base year value generally limited to no more than 2% (or the change in California CPI if lower)
  • Proposition 8 permits a temporary decline-in-value assessment where market value falls below the factored base year value
  • Assessed value must be the lower of current market value or adjusted base year value
