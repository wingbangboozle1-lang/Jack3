Jack in the Box NNN property | decision brief

Asset A · Central Los Angeles area · evidence snapshot 2026-11-13 · prepared for adviser review

Print using your browser's print command · landscape, one page

Current position: preserve both options; do not select sell or renew yet. Privately confirm the lease and notice calendar first. Then compare executable sale terms after tax with a negotiated renewal / hold case and the correlated downside risks. The reported expiry range of 2030–2035 is owner-supplied context, not a verified lease date or decision deadline.

What the evidence supports

  • Public city, county and metro evidence gives broad economic context; it is not a parcel valuation or a reliable local projection by itself.
  • Jack in the Box issuer filings describe system performance, not the lease guarantor or the subject store.
  • Both paths retain risk: hold carries tenant, lease-rollover and concentration exposure; sell carries transaction costs, tax and reinvestment risk.
  • Wildfire, earthquake and flood impacts can combine rent interruption, repairs, insurance constraints and reduced exit liquidity.

Decision gates · private work first

  1. Real-estate counsel: abstract the executed lease and amendments; verify expiry, options, notice windows, casualty / assignment provisions and legal obligor. Reconcile every date to a notice calendar.
  2. CPA / tax counsel: calculate sale proceeds after debt, costs, transfer taxes, basis, depreciation and applicable federal / state taxes; separately model any eligible exchange.
  3. Broker: obtain confidential written sale indications and enforceable renewal terms, then compare equivalent dates and assumptions.
  4. Engineer / insurance adviser: screen actual parcel hazards and policy terms; stress correlated downtime, repair and liquidity scenarios.
  5. Researcher: use the location only in a secure workflow for consistent tract, traffic, planning, school and competitor evidence.

Evidence boundaries

The public record does not contain the executed lease, exact parcel, store sales, rent schedule, tax basis, debt, offer, renewal quote, insurance policy or owner liquidity / concentration limits. No sale price, cap rate, after-tax figure, parcel hazard status, calibrated forecast or winner is asserted.

Privacy: keep identifiers, lease files and owner finances offline with authorized advisers; publish only aggregate, non-identifying findings. This page contains placeholders and no private inputs.
