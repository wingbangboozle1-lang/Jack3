Risk-balanced conclusion

Wealth preservation with growth optionality

The lowest-risk route is the one that keeps a choice available: market while contractual income is still in place, require written renewal terms, and cap how much of Owner A's net worth this single asset represents. Insurance verification and a re-letting reserve are prerequisites for either route. Redevelopment and a pure wait-until-expiry strategy carry the widest dispersion of outcomes and are not the default.
