09 / DISCOVERY REGISTER

Open questions & missing data

A decision cannot be reasonable while the facts that decide it are unknown. This is the register of what is missing, why each gap blocks a choice, and the exact step that closes it. Values are left blank rather than estimated.

How to read this register

20 open questions. 12 are private property or financial documents that no public source can supply; 7 are official records that exist but were not retrieved for this file because retrieving them would identify the parcel. The distinction matters: the first group needs the owner to act, the second needs one records request.

Every row carries its source link. Where the row records an absence, the link goes to the official register that would hold the fact - the Assessor, the Recorder, the zoning portal, FEMA, CAL FIRE, SEC EDGAR - and the row text states that the value was not retrieved. A link is a route to an answer, not evidence that the answer is known.

No personal data. The register names the class of document and the role that holds it (owner, franchisee, broker, CPA) and never a person, address or parcel identifier. Completing it requires private documents that are deliberately not collected here.

Order of work. Q-01 to Q-06 decide whether a sale is even the right track; Q-07 to Q-11 decide the after-tax net and the land option; Q-12 to Q-15 decide the pricing and demand evidence; Q-16 to Q-20 are the documentary and contractual checks that can invalidate any of the above.
