Order of work

The shortest route to a defensible decision
  1. Read the lease. A real-estate attorney abstracts the executed lease and amendments: Q-01, Q-02, Q-03, Q-12.
  2. Fix the place privately. Jurisdiction, tax bill, zoning and the three hazard viewers: Q-06, Q-11, Q-13, Q-16.
  3. Ask the tenant in writing. Estoppel, site sales and renewal intention, with a response date: Q-04, Q-05.
  4. Fix the owner's numbers. Basis, depreciation, structure, debt and goals with the CPA and advisers: Q-08, Q-09, Q-10.
  5. Test the market. Two or three independent price opinions and comparable sales: Q-07, Q-19.
  6. Check the downside. Insurance, building condition and environmental history: Q-14, Q-15.
  7. Compare. After-tax sale proceeds against the hold and extension cases, and against what the proceeds would earn elsewhere: Q-18.
