The NNN value equation

Value = Contract rent ÷ Cap rate. Your only levers: (i) the rent number and its escalators, (ii) the cap rate the market assigns, (iii) the term’s remaining length. You control rent negotiations directly (at renewal); cap rates you don’t; term you control only by acting early. This is why the entire strategy on this site points to early, documented renewal negotiation with sale optionality.

Compliance & file checklist (all internal — never published)

  1. Lease abstract — expiry date, option(s) count & length, notice window (often 6–12 months — calendar it with 18-month early reminder), escalation steps, percentage rent, co-tenancy (n/a single-tenant), exclusives, demolition rights, holdover rate.
  2. Estoppel certificate — executed, dated, from tenant AND guarantor if any; confirms no defaults, correct dates, current rent.
  3. Insurance — current COI; limits ≥ lease minimums; additional insured; terrorism/riot; tenant business interruption (your income depends on their operations).
  4. Taxes — LA County base 1% + voter bonds; post-sale Prop 13 reset analysis for your CPA; Assessor link: assessor.lacounty.gov ↗
  5. Seismic — order structural letter (LA non-ductile concrete / URM context); verify tenant’s repair obligation for code compliance in lease §
  6. Title & ALTA — update ALTA/NSPS survey; check easements, drive-through stacking, signage rights (pole sign rights are valuable for QSR)
  7. Permits & zoning — verify drive-through entitlement survives any closure/change of use; ZIMAS ↗
  8. Environmental — Phase I ESA at sale/refinance; dry-cleaning/auto adjacency screen
  9. Entity & estate — titled in LLC? successor trust? insurance within the entity? Depreciation schedule current? CPA review annually
