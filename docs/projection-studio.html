Projection Studio — Renew vs. Sell model

Interactive present-value comparison of (A) selling today vs (B) holding and renewing through the expiry window and beyond. All defaults are illustrative placeholders, not your lease terms. Replace them with your lease abstract and broker quotes; nothing here is a verified market value or investment advice.

ℹ️
How it works. Scenario A capitalizes next year’s base rent at the exit cap rate (less transaction costs) — i.e., today’s implied price — and reinvests the net proceeds at the reinvestment rate. Scenario B models escalated rent through the expiry, applies the renewal bump at the reset date, then values the property at sale at the exit cap rate on forward NOI, and discounts everything at the discount rate (your opportunity cost of capital). The sensitivity table shows Scenario B−A present-value advantage across exit cap rates (rows) × renewal bumps (columns); green = holding wins, red = selling wins.
