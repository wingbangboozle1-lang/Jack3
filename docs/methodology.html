Methodology, Sources & Privacy

How every number on this site was produced, verified, and sourced — and the privacy rules under which the work was done.

Verification protocol (line-by-line)

  1. Every table row on this site is tagged VERIFIED · OFFICIAL SOURCE (link to a .gov / SEC / state source), INDUSTRY · NON-GOVERNMENTAL (brokerage/commissioned studies — never presented as official), or FLAGGED · UNVERIFIED (confirmed blank or approximate).
  2. If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the cell is left as “—” or an explicit “unverified” note and the item is reproduced on Flagged for Review with a remediation action. No value was invented, interpolated, or “estimated” without a label.
  3. Where two official agencies publish the same fact with different methodologies (e.g., Census decennial vs DOF projections), both are shown and the discrepancy is disclosed in Flagged for Review.
  4. Derived figures (e.g., 2021→2025 HPI change +28.4%; 2025→2035 population change −1.5%) are computed from the cited official series and labeled as computed.
  5. Analysis, judgment and recommendations are labeled as such and are not “verified data.”

Nothing from any preserved tree was deleted; duplicates were folded into the canonical site only after verification.

Privacy statement

🔒
No personal information appears on this site. The property is described only as “a NNN-leased Jack in the Box restaurant in Central Los Angeles (lease expiring 2030–2035).” No owner identity, address, parcel number, rent roll, loan, entity name, listing link, or tax data is published anywhere in this repository or the live site. Any personal information encountered during research was disregarded for publication purposes.
