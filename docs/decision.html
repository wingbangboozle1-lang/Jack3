Projection method (run once lease + site data exist)

  1. Remaining-term rent schedule (contractual) → discount at risk-adjusted rate anchored to Fed funds + spreads.
  2. Renewal scenarios — renew at market / renew at discount / dark — weighted by site sales and guarantor strength.
  3. Residual land value (redevelopment) using LADCP zoning + appraisal comps.
  4. Hazard haircut: earthquake/casualty insurance + business-interruption assumptions (broker).
  5. Compare NPV of A–E net of taxes and transaction costs; choose the highest risk-adjusted NPV matching [OWNER]'s tolerance.
