  • Puente Hills blind-thrust runs beneath downtown LA & East LA — your corridor is above it. A full-rupture scenario implies $82B–$252B total economic loss (USGS). A Northridge-repeat (M6.7) is the calibration event: 57 deaths, tens of billions in losses.
  • USGS 30-year regional exceedance probabilities (UCERF3): 60% ≥ M6.7 · 46% ≥ M7.0 · 31% ≥ M7.5 in the LA region — elevated but not imminent; price it via insurance verification, not panic. FEMA National Risk Index: LA County is one of the highest expected-annual-loss and lowest-resilience scores in the nation (quoted in LA County’s 2025 All-Hazards Mitigation Plan adoption motion, Sep 9, 2025).
  • Single-story masonry/URM and non-ductile concrete buildings have elevated collapse risk; LA requires retrofits (URM already mandated; non-ductile concrete retrofit ordinance in effect). Confirm the building’s class and retrofit status with a structural engineer — do not assume.
  • NNN angle: tenant repairs many items, but seismic code compliance and insurance gaps are lease-specific — abstract § casualty, § insurance, § compliance now. Loss-of-rents / business-interruption coverage for the tenant is your income protection.

  • Smoke impacts on restaurant trade are real but transient; expect demand dips during smoke events; delivery pivot mitigates.

☢️
Concentration + tail risk, stated plainly. This asset concentrates earthquake (Puente Hills), tenant distress (JACK credit), and lease-expiry (term decay) risks in one line. The NNN structure transfers operating costs to the tenant but does not transfer seismic damage to the building, tenant credit, or term decay. Mitigate by: early renewal, verified insurance, seismic confirmation, and — if any trigger fires — selling into the 2026–2028 Olympic window with full documentation.
