What the numbers mean

  • Labour market: LA MSA unemployment 4.4% (Sep 2026, NSA) — better than California (5.1% SA) and slightly above the US (4.2% SA). The 2026 trend: May 4.8 → Jun 5.0 → Jul 5.0 → Aug 4.8 → Sep 4.4; easing, not deteriorating. Payrolls flat at ~6.30M — no recession signal, no boom either.
  • Consumer scale: $44.0B food & accommodation sales countywide (2022) and $81.35B retail sales in the city (2022, $21,281 per capita). The operating pond for a QSR is enormous; capture rate, not market size, is the question.
  • Chain-area jobs base: 3.99M county payroll jobs across 304,988 employer establishments (2023), +2.3% employment growth 2022→2023 — modest but positive.
  • Commute burden: 30.4–30.7 minutes average — heavy car dependence favors drive-through; weekday lunch/dinner dayparts near employment nodes.
⚠️
Watch item. LA metro payrolls are essentially flat (6,311K Jun → 6,300K Sep 2026) while California unemployment sits a full point above the national rate. A flat-to-soft 2026–27 is a headwind for same-store sales — exactly what JACK reported (system SSS −1.1% Q3 FY2026). Underwrite the renewal on contractual escalators, not on sales growth.
