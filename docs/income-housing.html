Read-through

  • Affordability squeeze: city median rent $1,933/mo vs median household income $81,939/yr → housing ≈ 28% of gross income before tax; homeownership rate just 36.0% in the city. Cost-pressed consumers trade into value QSR — a structural tailwind for Jack in the Box’s price positioning, but they are also quick to cut frequency (ticket elasticity).
  • Poverty: 16.5% city / 13.3% county — value messaging matters; expect deep deal usage, which pressures restaurant-level margins (JACK FY2025 restaurant margin 19.6%).
  • Asset appreciation: FHFA HPI LA County +28.4% (2021→2025), +1.7% YoY 2025 (403.74 vs 2000=100). Replacement-cost reality: land + construction in infill LA is expensive — your NNN rent stream is backed by very high-cost dirt, which supports residual land value at expiry.
  • Rent inflation vs wage inflation: gross rents up sharply since 2020 while wages lag → consumer strain persists into 2027+; conservative underwriting advised.
🏠
Why this matters for a NNN decision. The Property’s exit value at expiry is bounded below by land residual value, which in infill Central LA is elevated by housing scarcity — the same scarcity that squeezes tenants. Selling into a scarcity premium now vs. riding the renewal bump is the crux; see Decision Analysis.
