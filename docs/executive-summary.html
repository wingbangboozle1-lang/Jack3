Executive Summary

Decision brief: whether to sell a single-tenant, NNN-leased Jack in the Box restaurant in Central Los Angeles before its lease expires (2030–2035), or renew/extend the lease — assessed against current economic activity, verified official data, and a structured risk register. Property and owner are deliberately anonymized throughout this site.

🔒
Privacy first. No owner name, no street address, no parcel number, no listing references, and no personal financial data appear anywhere on this site or in this repository. All research was conducted on public, official sources only. See the Methodology & Privacy page.

This is a single-tenant, single-market, triple-net retail asset: passive income today, but with material remaining-term, tenant-credit and interest-rate exposure. The lease’s 2030–2035 expiry window is the pivotal fact: NNN assets lose value as the term burns down, and the window between now and expiry is when leverage over renewal terms is greatest.

2 · What the official data says

  • Population is flat-to-declining. California DOF projects LA County falling from 9.85M (2025) to 9.71M (2035), 9.64M (2040) and 8.96M (2060) — see Population Projections.
  • Density is extreme. LA city density is 8,304 people/sq mi with 47.2% Hispanic/Latino population and a very large food-away-from-home market: $17.4B restaurant & food-service sales in the city (2022) (Economy & Jobs).
  • Labor market is soft but not in recession. LA MSA unemployment 4.4% (Sep 2026) vs US 4.2% and California 5.1%; payrolls 6.30M, flat (Economy & Jobs).
  • Income is mid-tier for coastal CA. Median household income $81,939 (city) / $90,112 (county); 16.5% city poverty (Income & Housing).
  • Schools improving. LAUSD graduation rate 86.5% (2025), chronic absenteeism down to 21.9% from 39.8% in 2022 — still elevated (Schools & Education).
  • Housing costs are severe. Median gross rent $1,933/mo; median owner value $921,200 (city) (Income & Housing) — wages vs housing mismatch is the area’s structural affordability problem.
  • Tenant is stressed but de-leveraging. JACK FY2025 revenue $1.465B (−6.7%); system SSS −4.2% in FY2025 and −1.1% in Q3 FY2026; stock ~72% below FY2020; Del Taco sold (Dec 2025, ~$119M) and proceeds applied to debt; $110M note prepayment and $500M refinance completed; targeting Net Debt/EBITDA 4–5x; 150–200 store closure program underway (Tenant & Brand).
  • Hazards are real. Jan 2025 fires destroyed ~16,000 structures region-wide; Puente Hills blind-thrust full-rupture scenario: $82–252B losses (Hazards & Black Swans).
  • 2028 Olympics are a once-in-a-generation demand catalyst inside the holding window (SCAG estimate: $13.6–17.6B regional GDP add, 2024–2029) — Growth Catalysts.

3 · Options considered

Option A — Sell before expiry (2026–2028). Capture remaining-term premium now; crystallize gain; redeploy (e.g., 1031 exchange). Cons: sells the 2028–2035 renewal upside; locks today’s cap-rate environment (net-lease avg 6.9% retail; QSR tighter, ~5–6% industry estimate); taxes.

Option B — Renew and hold through expiry and beyond. Keeps passive NNN income; rent bump at renewal resets value; tax basis protected; inflation passed through. Cons: concentration; tenant credit risk; term/value decay if renewal falters; illiquidity.

Option C — Hybrid (recommended): negotiate renewal/extension NOW with an escalator reset, while keeping the asset market-ready. Retains upside, preserves optionality, forces the tenant to reveal intentions early. Sell only if pre-set triggers are breached.

✅
Recommendation. Do not fire-sale today — but do not drift toward expiry either. Execute the Option C playbook: (1) commission a lease abstract + estoppel now; (2) open renewal talks 24–36 months pre-expiry targeting a +10–20% reset plus NNN re-affirmation (illustrative targets — verify against your lease and market quotes); (3) keep a sale-ready package and 2 broker opinions of value on file; (4) set hard triggers for selling (below). If the tenant cannot or will not meet the reset, list 2027–2028 — inside the Olympic-catalyst window and with years of term remaining. Full logic and weighted scoring on Decision Analysis; PV modeling on Projection Studio.

4 · Decision triggers (any one → move from “hold/renew” to “sell”)

  1. Tenant declines renewal/extension or demands flat reset (no escalator catch-up).
  2. Two consecutive quarters of deteriorating system SSS worse than −3% or subject-store sales coverage <1.2× rent (coverage not publicly verifiable — obtain internally).
  3. JACK net debt/EBITDA not trending toward the 4–5x target, or any 8-K signaling liquidity distress.
  4. Unsolicited bid at ≥ +15% over the broker opinion of value, or net-lease QSR cap rates compress ≥75 bps below today’s quotes.
  5. Significant capital requirement emerges (seismic retrofit mandate, roof, parking, ADA) exceeding 12 months of rent.

5 · Risk posture

Balanced BB-style profile: strong infill location and NNN structure, offset by single-asset concentration and tenant credit. All 14 tracked risks — likelihood × impact, mitigants, BAU vs black-swan — are on the Risk Register; hazard quantification on Hazards & Black Swans. Manageable with: early renewal, insurance certificates, seismic verification, diversified redeployment of any sale proceeds, and liquidity buffer.
