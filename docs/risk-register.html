Risk Register

Every decision-relevant risk, scored, mitigated, and categorized into business-as-usual (expected variance) and black-swan (rare, severe) events. Likelihood and impact are analytical judgments; factual anchors are sourced on the linked pages.

🧮
Scoring. Score = likelihood × impact (1–25). Critical ≥ 15 · High 10–14 · Medium 5–9 · Low ≤ 4. The register balances wealth preservation (mitigating R-02/R-09/R-12) against opportunity (rejecting an over-discounted forced sale).

Portfolio-level view

  • Dominant exposures: remaining-term value cliff (R-02, score 16) and tenant-closure risk (R-01, 15) — both mitigated by the Option C playbook (act by 2028, not at expiry).
  • Tail exposures: earthquake (R-09, 10) and civil unrest (R-13, 8) — capped by insurance verification + seismic confirmation; retention of risk is explicit and sized.
  • Diversification is the only cure for concentration (R-14, 15): if you cannot hold the concentration comfortably, the rational endpoint is a 1031 into diversified income real estate — price it with two broker opinions before deciding.
