LAUSD trajectory

  • Graduation rate: 82.1% (2019) → 86.0% (2022) → 83.6% (2023) → 86.7% (2024) → 86.5% (2025) — sustained improvement vs pre-pandemic baseline.
  • Chronic absenteeism: 18.2% (2019) → 39.8% (2022) → 31.0% (2023) → 23.3% (2024) → 21.9% (2025) — crisis improved but not normalized; remains above the 2019 baseline.
  • Attainment: 79.6% of LA city adults hold a HS diploma (county 80.9%); 38.5% hold a bachelor’s degree (county 36.0%) — a highly service- and trade-oriented workforce, consistent with QSR employment availability and consumer mix.
🎓
Read-through. School quality is a second-order driver of household formation and retail spending in family neighborhoods. The improvement in LAUSD metrics is genuinely positive for the corridor’s long-run residential value — but the Property’s performance is far more sensitive to tenant credit and traffic than to school metrics. Do not overweight this factor in the sell/renew decision; it matters for resale liquidity (family buyers) more than for operations.
