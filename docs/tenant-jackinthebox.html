Credit assessment — what the official filings reveal

  • Scale: ~2,115 restaurants (Jul 2026), predominantly franchised (93% franchise by count). Franchise-level margin 39.9% (FY2025) — a healthy royalty/rent machine. Franchise rental revenues $368.6M (FY2025) flow from franchisees like the likely guarantor at your store — check whether YOUR tenant is corporate JACK or a franchisee (see estoppel step).
  • Deterioration: FY2025 revenue −6.7% YoY; system SSS −4.2% (FY2025), −6.7% (Q1 FY2026), −1.1% (Q3 FY2026); stock $100→$28 (FY2020→FY2025) vs S&P 500 $100→$214; FY2025 dividends discontinued.
  • Active repair: Del Taco divested (Dec 2025, ~$119M) to retire ~$105M debt; $110M Series 2019-1 prepayment + $500M 2026-1 refinancing (Q3 FY2026); $200M+ additional paydown targeted over 1–2 years; Net Debt/EBITDA target 4–5x; closure program removing underperforming (mostly 30+-year-old) units — roughly 86 closed in FY2025 plus more slated.
  • Governance strain: activist investor (Biglari, ~9.9% per press reports — FLAGGED, verify EDGAR) and poison pill (Jul 2025 — FLAGGED); CEO transition to Lance Tucker (Mar 2025, reported) then Mark King as Interim CEO (Aug 2026).
  • Interpretation (analysis, not filing fact): JACK is a stressed but solvent, free-cash-flowing franchisor actively shrinking the system to heal the balance sheet. For a landlord this cuts both ways: stronger surviving estate, but elevated probability the subject store is among those evaluated for closure — especially if it is an older unit in a soft trade area. This probability is not publicly quantifiable — see Flagged for Review.

Franchise vs corporate lease — the critical distinction

🔑
Most Jack in the Box restaurants are franchisee-operated. A lease to a franchisee is not a lease to JACK Inc. unless guaranteed. Your estoppel step must determine: (1) guarantor entity (corporate JACK / franchisee LLC / both); (2) guarantee type (full unconditional vs limited); (3) rent coverage (store sales vs rent — internal); (4) whether the subject store is in or near the 150–200-store closure list (ask in writing); (5) SNDA from lender; (6) notice and cure rights. None of this is obtainable from public filings — it comes from the lease file. Do it now.
