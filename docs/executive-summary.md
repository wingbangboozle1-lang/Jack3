## The question

A single-tenant **Jack in the Box** quick-service restaurant in **central Los Angeles, CA**, held under a **triple-net (NNN) lease expiring sometime in 2030–2035**. Decision: **sell before expiry or renew/hold** — weighing geography, income, schools, population, job growth, and every factor that moves the local economy, with risks for every option including black swans (wildfire, earthquake, flood), all from verified official sources.

## The recommendation (base case)

**Sell before the lease expires — target a 2027–2029 sale window — and execute the sale as an IRS §1031 like-kind exchange into replacement property(ies) in lower-hazard, faster-growing markets.** If (and only if) a new lease/extension of **≥10 years** at or above current rent with escalations is signed **now** by a creditworthy tenant (ideally with a corporate guarantee), holding becomes the better option. Otherwise the expiry cliff dominates. Set a hard decision deadline no later than **24 months before the exact expiry date** (conservatively ≈ end of 2031 using the 2033 midpoint of the 2030–2035 window).
