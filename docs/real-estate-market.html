Net-lease mechanics you must internalize

  • Remaining-term gravity. Net-lease pricing embeds remaining term: as the lease approaches expiry with no renewal in hand, buyer demand thins and implied cap rates widen. The 2030–2035 window is the danger zone — value erosion is nonlinear.
  • Cap rate math. At a 6.9% net-lease average (industry), $1 of net rent ≈ $14.49 of value; at 7.5% ≈ $13.33 (−8.0%); at 6.0% ≈ $16.67 (+15.0%). Every +100 bps costs roughly 12–13% of value at these levels. Rate trajectory (10Y 4.90% as of 2026-11-04) is therefore a first-order sale-timing variable — see Projection Studio.
  • QSR premium positioning. QSR NNN deals frequently trade tighter than the blended retail average (~5–6% per third-party reporting — FLAGGED, not primary). Branded, drive-through, corporate-guaranteed JACK assets would normally command that tighter range if the tenant and term are strong; JACK’s current credit trajectory argues for underwriting the retail average or wider until proven otherwise.
  • Escalation compounding. A stacked 8%-per-5-years structure compounds to ~1.55%/yr — modest real protection vs 3–4% inflation; renewal resets are where the value is made.
🏷️
Pricing honesty. Any specific price, cap rate or rent figure for the subject asset is deliberately absent — no official valuation exists, and listing data is excluded (privacy + not official). To underwrite a sale, obtain two signed broker opinions of value; to underwrite a hold, abstract the lease and model the renewal reset. Nothing on this page should be read as an appraisal.
