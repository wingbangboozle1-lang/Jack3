Catalyst inventory

🏟️ 2028 Olympic & Paralympic Games

Paralympic Games Aug 15–27, 2028 (verified, IPC); Olympic Games July 2028 (dates reported 14–30 July — not re-verified, Flagged F-16). LA Memorial Coliseum / Downtown & regional venues. SCAG (governmental): $13.6–17.6B added regional GDP 2024–2029 (six counties). LAEDC commissioned study (non-governmental): $20–40B output. LA28 budget ~$7.15B (reported). Not a measurement — an estimate.

Property impact: visitor surge 2027–2028 around venues; downtown/East-LA corridors see traffic spikes; retail leasing and consumer spending bump. One-time pulse, not a permanent lift — but it lands inside your sale window.

🚇 Transit & infrastructure buildout

Metro rail/BRT expansion and LAX modernization are budgeted — check metro.net and flylax.com for official project scope/costs before relying on any number. No unverified figure is asserted on this page.

Property impact: accessibility improvements favor urban infill retail; construction-phase disruption is a short-term drag.

🏗️ Housing construction permits (2025: 22,250 countywide)

Countywide permits ticked at 22,250 (2025, QuickFacts) — active pipeline, mostly multifamily infill; net-new residents support local QSR counts even in a flat-population county (housing churn within LA is high — 90.8% of county residents live in the same house 1yr on, meaning 9.2% move annually).

🌆 Downtown/East LA revitalization & adaptive reuse

Office-to-residential conversions and streetscape programs (City Planning, planning.lacity.org) — monitor ZIMAS/council files for the specific corridor. No quantity asserted as verified.

⏱️
Timing insight. The Olympics catalyst peaks 2027–2029 — ahead of the 2030 expiry edge and squarely in the 2026–2028 sell window. If you sell, sell into that strength with a fresh lease or strong estoppel in hand. If you renew, the catalyst still benefits tenant sales and your escalations. Either way, do not let the expiry negotiation drift past early 2028.
