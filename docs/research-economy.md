## 8. Summary assessment of area fundamentals

- **Demand base: strong and durable** — 3.87M city / 9.69M county residents, high density, $81B city retail sales, $17.4B food-service sales, low unemployment, +8.7% projected job growth to 2032 (EDD).
- **Growth: flat-to-declining population** per the state's official DOF projections through and beyond the 2030–2035 lease-expiry window; SCAG diverges (flagged).
- **Affordability/inequality:** median HH income $81.9k (city), 16.5% poverty (city) — value-segment QSR demand is supported; premium concepts face a tougher market.
- **Hazard load: highest in the nation** (FEMA NRI 100.0; USGS 60% M6.7+/30 yr; 2025 wildfire map expansion; stressed insurance market). Under an NNN lease the *tenant* pays insurance and taxes, so direct cost is shifted — but owner retains casualty damage to structure/land, loss-of-rent, and re-tenanting risk at expiry.
