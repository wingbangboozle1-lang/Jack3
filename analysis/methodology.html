Verification standard

  1. One claim, one source. Each row in the dataset names a single source page. If two sources disagree, both are recorded and the conflict is disclosed on the Flagged page rather than resolved silently.
  2. Read it, then cite it. A value is marked Verified only when it was read on the linked page during compilation on 2026-11-06. Where a number came from a search snippet and the page could not be read, it is marked Unverified.
  3. No inference. Nothing is filled in by calculation unless the row says Calculated and shows the formula. Nothing is filled in from a secondary summary unless the source is itself named and tiered.
  4. Parcel-level claims are not made. The address is withheld, so seismic, flood, fire-hazard and zoning determinations require a parcel lookup and are published as open items (F-03, F-04, F-05, F-09).
  5. Judgement is labelled. Likelihood and impact scores in the risk register, the weights in the decision matrix and the model's assumptions are the analyst's judgement. They are shown in full so a reader can substitute their own.

Privacy and anonymisation

No personal information is published. The following are withheld from the website: the street address and parcel number ([PROPERTY-A]), the owner's name and any individual's name ([OWNER-1]), the franchisee's identity and principals ([FRANCHISEE-1]), lease counterparties, and any contact, financial-account or tenancy information. No photograph, map pin, or data field on this site can be used to identify the parcel or a person. Everything published is area-level and available to any member of the public.

This site is public. Any information that could identify the property or a person has been kept out of the repository itself, not merely hidden in the presentation, so that the published product cannot leak it through source files.

Model method

The scenario model is a deterministic annual cash-flow projection. It computes value as NOI ÷ cap rate, projects rent at a fixed growth rate, applies a structural reserve, exits at a stated cap rate with stated sale costs, and solves the internal rate of return by bisection. The sell-and-reinvest path applies the same machinery to net proceeds reinvested at the franchisee-QSR benchmark. The script is tools/model.py and its output is assets/data/scenarios.json, which is what the site renders. Nothing on the site is typed in by hand after the script runs.

Limitations, stated plainly

  • The property is not valued. Without rent and NOI there is no valuation, and none is asserted.
  • Submarket data is coarse. Verified figures are county, city or metro level; no ZIP-level table could be pulled (F-13).
  • Industry data is industry data. Cap rates come from a research firm, not a government body. They are labelled.
  • Hazard figures are scenarios, not forecasts. The USGS ShakeOut numbers describe a modelled event in 2008 dollars.
  • No forecast of prices. The model shows what happens under stated assumptions; it does not predict the market.

Reproduce or challenge anything here

Every number is traceable. The dataset lives in assets/data/dataset.json, the sources in assets/data/sources.json, the risks in assets/data/risks.json, and the flags in assets/data/flagged.json. Rebuild the site with python3 tools/build.py; recompute the model with python3 tools/model.py; check every link with python3 tools/linkcheck.py. Task ownership and the verification log are in COORDINATION.md.

Privacy: the parcel is anonymised as [PROPERTY-A]; owner, franchisee and all personal information are withheld.
