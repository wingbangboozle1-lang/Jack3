What the numbers mean for [PROPERTY-A]

The contraction is deliberate

38 of the 47 units closed in Q4 were shut under the "JACK on Track" block-closure programme. Management is pruning underperformers rather than retreating. That matters: a unit that is performing is more likely to be renewed, so the question is whether [PROPERTY-A]'s unit is a pruner or a keeper. Only the franchisor's unit-level data can answer that, and it is not public.

Same-store sales are the warning light

−4.2% for the year and −7.4% in Q4, attributed in the filing to fewer transactions and unfavourable mix, partly offset by menu price. A unit losing transactions is a unit whose rent coverage is falling even if its rent is flat. Under a triple-net lease the owner does not see this until renewal, which is exactly why the extension conversation must start now rather than in 2030.

Corporate is reshaping, not expanding

The Del Taco divestiture at $115 million, plus a guided unit count of 2,050–2,100 for fiscal 2026 (from 2,136), signals a management team focused on the balance sheet. New-unit development in an expensive infill market like central Los Angeles is unlikely to be a priority. The base case should be renewal of an existing, profitable unit or vacancy — not relocation of a new corporate build.

Credit quality depends on who signed

If the lease is with Jack in the Box Inc. with a corporate guarantee, the credit is the public company. If it is with a franchisee — the common structure, since the system is roughly 93% franchised — the credit is a private operator whose financials are not public. This single fact can move the achievable cap rate by 50–100 basis points.

Where the lease sits in this

The lease expires between 2030 and 2035: five to nine years from now. That window has three useful properties:

  1. It is long enough to negotiate from strength. An extension agreed now — while the unit is trading — is worth more to both sides than a renewal negotiation conducted in the shadow of a closure decision.
  2. It is short enough that the exit cap rate already reflects lease-term risk. A buyer of a five-year QSR lease discounts for rollover; the Boulder Group reports the widest pricing uncertainty exactly in shorter-term and unrated-operator assets.
  3. It overlaps the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles (14–30 July 2028, S23), a demand and rent event that sits inside the remaining term rather than after it.
