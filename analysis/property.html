Transfer taxes and the cost of a sale

In the City of Los Angeles the documentary transfer tax is the county rate of $1.10 per $1,000 plus a city rate of $4.50 per $1,000, a combined 0.56% of price (S14). Add roughly 3% brokerage and 1% legal and closing, and a sale costs about 4.5% of price before any Measure ULA surtax and before tax on the gain.

The Measure ULA cliff

Measure ULA applies only inside the City of Los Angeles and only above a price threshold. Because the threshold is indexed every 1 July, the rate table must be re-confirmed for the closing date — which is why the two ULA rows in the dataset are marked unverified (see flag F-01).

Lease economics under a triple-net structure

What NNN does for the owner

  • The tenant pays property tax, insurance and maintenance, so the owner's cash flow is close to net.
  • The owner is therefore exposed to credit risk, not operating risk — which is why the tenant's trajectory above matters more than the building's condition.
  • At expiry all of that reverts to the owner. A roof, parking lot, HVAC and signage package can easily absorb 12–24 months of net income.

What the model assumes, and why

  • 2% of NOI a year held back as a structural reserve, even under NNN, because the reserve is needed the day the tenant leaves.
  • 4.5% sale cost: 3.0% brokerage, 0.56% transfer, about 0.94% legal and closing. ULA is added only when the price crosses the threshold.
  • 2.5% rent growth, between a 2% fixed bump and a 10%-every-five-years structure amortised.

Section 1031 and the tax deferral option

A like-kind exchange defers gain, it does not eliminate it: the taxpayer must identify replacement property in writing within 45 days and close within 180 days (S15). Unrecaptured Section 1250 gain is taxed at up to 25% and long-term capital gain on real property at up to 20%, before any state tax (S16). A 1031 exchange preserves the deferred gain inside the replacement asset, so the comparison on the Scenarios page is run pre-tax, with the after-tax illustration shown separately and labelled.

The binding constraint on selling is not the market — it is the tax and friction. An owner with a low basis who sells without exchanging can lose 15–25% of the price to tax on top of 4.5% in fees. Any sell decision has to be made jointly with a CPA, not after the fact.
