How to read this for the decision

What is supportive

  • Leisure and hospitality is hiring (+1.5% year over year, 782,000 jobs). A restaurant market that is adding jobs is a restaurant market that can absorb a re-let.
  • Education and health services is the growth engine (+3.8%, 1.34 million jobs) — stable, non-cyclical daytime demand that suits a drive-through on a commuter corridor.
  • Income and rents are high by national standards ($90,112 median household income; $1,954 median gross rent), which supports menu pricing and therefore rent coverage.
  • County property values are still rising (+4.42%, 16th straight year) even after the fires.

What is not supportive

  • Population is shrinking: −3.2% since April 2020. A falling population is the single most important long-run headwind for a consumer-facing site.
  • Unemployment is 4.4% and total nonfarm employment grew only 0.5% — the metro is not in a boom.
  • Information (−5.7%) and professional services (−0.5%) are contracting, which thins weekday lunch traffic in office-adjacent corridors.
  • Inflation at 3.7% against lease escalations that are often 2% is a real-terms rent cut every year.
