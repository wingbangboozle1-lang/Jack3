Tier 1 is a government, statutory or court source. Tier 2 is a filed or official issuer disclosure. Tier 3 is industry research and is used only for market context.

Tier definitions

Tier 1 · Official

Federal and state agencies, county and city departments, statutes and the Secretary of State. Used for every factual claim about demographics, employment, prices, taxes, hazards and law.

Tier 2 · Filings

SEC filings and the official Games organiser. Legally accountable disclosure, audited where applicable. Used for everything about the tenant.

Tier 3 · Industry

Net-lease research and broker market data. Never used for a legal or statutory fact, and always labelled "Industry data" in the dataset.

Sources that were attempted and could not be used

  • Census data API. The API now requires a registered key, so ZIP-code-level tables could not be pulled programmatically. County and city QuickFacts pages were used instead (F-13).
  • BLS QCEW API. The county-by-industry CSV endpoint returned an error; sector detail was taken from the BLS Economy at a Glance page instead.
  • City of Los Angeles Office of Finance — Measure ULA. The deep link returned 404; the rate table is therefore flagged (F-01).
  • Boulder Group PDFs. The report landing page and its executive summaries were read; the PDFs themselves were not parsed in this session.
