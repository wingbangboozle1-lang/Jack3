Recommendation in one paragraph

Do not sell before the lease expires on an unconditional basis. Hold, extend and prepare, and sell only when a defined trigger fires. The arithmetic in the model favours holding: under the base case an unlevered hold returns 8.03% a year against 7.25% for selling now and deferring tax through a Section 1031 exchange, and holding only loses if the exit cap rate widens to 7.22% or more. Selling costs 4.5% in fees before any tax — and, above the $5.4 million threshold, another 4% to 5.5% in Measure ULA. Against that, the tenant is visibly contracting, which is the one fact that can destroy the hold case; so the recommendation is a conditional hold with a hard decision gate in 2029, not a passive one. Executive summary

1 · The question and what actually drives the answer

  • Three variables dominate: net operating income, the exit cap rate, and whether the tenant renews. All three are private. NOI and rent are unknown and are published as open items, not estimated.
  • Everything else — demographics, employment, schools, hazards — moves those three variables at the margin. They are necessary context, not the decision itself.
  • Two certain costs push toward holding: transaction friction of about 4.5% of price, and capital-gains plus depreciation-recapture tax that a 1031 exchange defers but does not eliminate.

2 · What the area data says

  • Mixed, mildly positive for a restaurant site. Leisure and hospitality employment is up 1.5% year over year and education/health up 3.8%, while information is down 5.7% and professional services down 0.5% (BLS).
  • Population is falling (−3.2% since 2020) and unemployment is 4.4% — a soft, not collapsing, market.
  • Household income is $90,112 county-wide and $81,939 in the city; median gross rent $1,954; poverty 13.3% county / 16.5% city (Census).
  • The county assessment roll grew 4.42% to $2.272 trillion despite the January 2025 fires — the 16th consecutive year of growth (Assessor).

3 · What the tenant data says

  • Jack in the Box closed 86 restaurants and opened 31 in fiscal 2025; same-store sales fell 4.2% for the year and 7.4% in Q4; guidance is 2,050–2,100 restaurants by fiscal 2026 year end versus 2,136 (SEC).
  • Del Taco is being sold for $115 million — management is reshaping the portfolio, not expanding it (10-K).
  • Implication: renewal is not automatic. The probability of a dark store at expiry is material, and that probability — not the demographics — is the main risk to the hold case.

4 · What the market says

  • Single-tenant net-lease cap rates rose to 6.92% in Q3 2026, the highest in over a decade; retail is 6.69%, corporate QSR 5.90%, franchisee QSR 6.87% (Boulder Group).
  • Financing costs now exceed net-lease cap rates — negative leverage — so the bid is dominated by all-cash buyers and pricing is soft. The 10-year Treasury sits at 4.90%.
  • Implication: this is a mediocre moment to sell income paper. It is a better moment to hold and prepare.
