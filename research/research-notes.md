## Decision framing (analysis, not verified fact)
- Sell vs renew at/near expiry 2030–2035.
- Key NNN market dynamic: value declines as remaining term shortens; near-term sale captures remaining-term premium; renewal preserves/extends term and restores value if escalators reset.
- Tenant fundamentals deteriorated (SSS negative through FY2025–Q3FY26; stock ~72% below FY2020) BUT balance sheet actively de-leveraging (~$215M+ debt retired in 2025–26 incl. $110M prepay + Del Taco proceeds; $500M refi) toward 4–5x Net Debt/EBITDA; brand closure program culls weak stores (tail risk of the subject store being among them must be assessed).
- LA County population declining per DOF projections → flat-to-negative demand growth; density, high traffic corridors mitigate for well-located infill QSR.
- Rates: 10Y at 4.90% → cap-rate-expansion risk on any sale; selling into 2026–2028 (pre-expiry, Olympic catalyst window) vs holding.
- Black swans: earthquake (Puente Hills $82–252B scenario), wildfire/smoke regional economic drag + insurance costs (Eaton/Palisades Jan 2025), recession, civil unrest, tenant bankruptcy/enfranchisee default; pandemics (QSR drive-through resilient per FY2020 experience — analysis, no figure cited).
