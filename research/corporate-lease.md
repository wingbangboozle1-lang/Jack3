## NNN lease context (structural facts for the decision)

- NNN = tenant pays base rent plus property taxes, insurance, and maintenance; landlord receives comparatively passive income but bears re-tenanting / residual-value risk at expiry. (General industry structure; lease-specific economics depend on the actual lease.)
- Key lease variables that ONLY the owner's lease documents can confirm: remaining term to 2030–2035, renewal options, rent escalations, guarantor (corporate vs franchisee), permitted use / exclusivity, demolition/redevelopment rights, condemnation and casualty clauses.
