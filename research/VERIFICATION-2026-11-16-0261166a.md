## Browser calculator: design and limits

`private-calculator.html` is generated as a self-contained page: its CSS and one inline JavaScript source are embedded, so the blank file can be downloaded and used offline. Twelve unnamed, blank-by-default numeric inputs cover a decision horizon, owner-selected discount rate, current written sale offer and cost/tax/debt line items, annual after-tax hold/renewal cash flows, and terminal net proceeds. No values are prefilled.

The only arithmetic is:

- **Sale-now net:** gross written price minus transaction costs, transfer taxes, debt/lien payoff, CPA-estimated income taxes and other costs.
- **Hold / renewal present value:** level annual after-tax cash flow received at each year-end, discounted at the user-entered rate, plus the terminal after-tax net proceeds discounted to the same date.

The model assumes level annual flows, no growth and a shared comparison horizon/rate. It does **not** calculate gain, adjusted basis, depreciation recapture, federal or California tax, NIIT, Measure ULA, county transfer tax, §1031 eligibility, future rent/exit value, hazard loss, or a recommendation. Current sale tax and transfer-tax amounts must come from qualified advisers / escrow; future proceeds and the discount rate are scenario inputs. No path is ranked or selected.

Privacy controls checked: no `form`, no `name` or `value` attributes on inputs, `autocomplete="off"`, one inline script only on this page, no remote resources, no network/storage/cookie APIs, a page CSP denying connections, no values in URLs, and an explicit clear action. The hosted page is public and ordinary page-access logs may exist; the page itself does not submit or persist input. Browser history/session-restore behavior is outside the page's control. The page advises against entering names, addresses, parcel IDs or account details and offers a blank offline copy.
