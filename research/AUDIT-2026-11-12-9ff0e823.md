## Requests for site integration / flagged review

1. **Unsupported universal discount-rate floor.** `projections.html` step 4 says use the 10-year Treasury as a floor for *any* discount rate. [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10) reports a dated yield; it does not prescribe a universal discount-rate floor. Recast as one dated benchmark and explicitly label the choice of discount rate as a scenario assumption, not a verified rule.
2. **Dormant model mismatch.** `scripts/projection.py` assigns `TREASURY_ANCHOR = 0.0486` while labeling the date 2026-11-09 and the narrative 4.88%. FRED reports 4.86% on November 6 and 4.88% on November 9. This module is not staged in the canonical public artifact, but downstream reuse would produce a mislabeled result. Fix and add an assertion against the dated anchor before reuse; do not assume the current public pages use this script.
3. **Decision gates are judgment, not verified thresholds.** The live landing page says “run both tracks now,” then schedules the sale process 24–36 months before an unknown earliest expiration; it also proposes a minimum ten-year extension. The exact scheduling and term gates are analyst choices, not established by the cited [issuer filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/807882/000080788226000090/ex991-q326earningsrelease.htm), and can conflict with notice deadlines, valuation, tax, or owner's objectives. Label them clearly as provisional examples; begin confidential lease and notice review now; determine actual marketing timing and acceptable renewal terms from the documents and after-tax comparison.
4. **Dedicated suggestions area missing from canonical navigation.** A decision matrix and repository review exist, but there is no distinct, prioritized site-improvement suggestions register with alternatives, tradeoffs, decision status and deferred implementation. Add one as a separate review-only section, source-linking evidence and marking suggestions as analysis, not official facts.
5. **Forecast and privacy limit.** A 13-step framework exists, but `projections.html` explicitly says no model was trained or back-tested and suppresses asset-level output. Without a privately verified location, lease and owner financials, neither a reliable asset forecast nor an individualized optimal decision can be established. Retain blank values, avoid publishing identifying details, and do not equate regional scenarios with parcel probability. [FEMA flood maps](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps), [California Geological Survey](https://www.conservation.ca.gov/cgs/geohazards/eq-zapp), and the [IRS like-kind exchange guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-estate-tax-tips) provide starting points for confidential due diligence, not substitutes for private documents.
