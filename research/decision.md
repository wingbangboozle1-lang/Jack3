## Options and risk/return character (qualitative; numbers need lease + appraisal)

| Option | Upside | Downside / Risk | Best when |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Sell well before expiry (bank term + options) | Locks in NNN premium pricing; transfers renewal, credit, and catastrophe risk; frees capital for 1031 exchange | Transaction costs; possible regret if renewed at higher rent; tax event if no exchange | Lease has short remaining term, weak escalations, or franchisee (non-corporate) guaranty |
| B. Renew early / blend-and-extend at market | Extends certain income; may fund reimage; preserves fee ownership in infill LA | Locks today's rent if market rises; tenant leverage near expiry; capex contributions | Site is high-performing, corporate-backed, infill location with redevelopment optionality preserved |
| C. Hold to expiry, decide then | Maximum information; LA28 demand data; rate path revealed | Wasting-asset decay: NNN value typically falls as term shortens; binary re-tenanting risk | Only with verified strong site sales + renewal options + casualty/condemnation protections |
| D. Sell with leaseback / redevelop | Captures land value in supply-constrained LA (8,304/sq mi density) | Entitlements, CEQA, capital intensity | Under-improved parcel, favorable zoning (verify at LADCP) |
| E. 1031 exchange into diversified NNN | Defers tax; diversifies tenant/geography/hazard risk | Exchange deadlines; replacement-property risk | Wealth-preservation priority with manageable risk |

## Preliminary directional read (subject to lease verification)

- The verified backdrop argues for **deciding early rather than drifting to expiry**: tenant system stress + hawkish rates + flat population + catastrophe overhang all penalize short-term NNN paper.
- **LA28 (2028) is a tactical window**: area demand/visibility peaks before the 2030–2035 expiry; a sale or blend-and-extend negotiated into that window likely prices better than one negotiated after it.
- Wealth-preservation + growth balance points to **A or E (monetize + redeploy/diversify)** unless the lease verifies as corporate-guaranteed with strong escalations and renewal options — in which case **B** is competitive.
- Nothing here overrides the actual lease: F7/F8 (terms, guarantor, options) are the highest-value verifications.
