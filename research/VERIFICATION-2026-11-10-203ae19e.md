## 4. Verification limits stated plainly

- **A verified row means the publisher displays that figure for that geography and period.** It never
  means the figure predicts this property, this trade area or this tenant's renewal decision.
- **No cap rate, rent, sale price, NOI, basis, debt, insurance term or renewal probability is published**,
  because none exists in the public record for this asset and the owner has not supplied one.
- **The break-even tables are arithmetic, not forecasts.** They invert the comparison so that the missing
  facts cancel; the assumptions are printed on the page next to the output.
- **Nothing in this pass required, stored or published personal information.** The asset remains
  *Asset A*; no address, parcel, owner, contact detail, lease or financial statement was collected.
- Rolling sources (BLS, FRED, Dashboard, QuickFacts) can be revised or re-vintaged after 2026-11-10.
