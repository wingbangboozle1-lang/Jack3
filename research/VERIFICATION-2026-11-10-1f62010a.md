# Line-by-line verification — 2026-11-10

## Scope and result

This is the final-integration audit of the **curated public root dashboard**. All 24 inherited official/issuer source pages were opened again, and six new official/issuer sources were checked. The 158 published evidence rows below were compared with the linked source text. `Verified` means the observation and its geography, period and label match that page; it does not mean the source predicts Asset A. `Analysis` is explicitly conditional reasoning, not an official value. `Unverified` has a blank value cell. `Needs review` records a limitation or irregularity and does not block publication.

**Result:** 158 rows across ten category pages; every row has a direct official or issuer link. Two old gaps were closed (county transfer tax and LAUSD graduation). No personal or parcel information was sought or added.

## Material corrections and irregularities

- Replaced the unsupported headline “every verified trend is flat or negative”: payrolls, education/health, leisure/hospitality, assessment roll and graduation outcomes include positive changes.
- Changed BLS “food at home” to the source’s actual **Food** category.
- Split mixed city/county demographic rows and the two wildfire rows so each published observation is supported by its own row-level link.
- Updated the 10-year Treasury observation from 4.84% (2026-11-05) to **4.86% (2026-11-06)**.
- The 2026 LAUSD Dashboard reports **86.2% four-year** and **87.6% combined four/five-year** graduation. These are different measures; the older 86.5% combined 2025 value must not be labeled four-year.
- The LA County Assessment Roll release says **4.42%** and **$96 billion** in its narrative but **“42%”** in one bullet. The site uses 4.42% and flags the official-page inconsistency.
- Added SCAG’s own adopted 2030 planning outputs with their model vintage and definitions. They are not our forecast and are not interpolated to 2035.
- Withheld `/advisory/` and `/dashboard/` from the public artifact: their extrapolated numerical scenarios/rankings are not established by their official citations. They remain repository-only audit material.
- Jack in the Box says FY2026 full-year results are scheduled for **2026-12-09**. Q3 FY2026 remains the latest results release in this snapshot; nothing is anticipated.
