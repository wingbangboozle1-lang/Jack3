Decision at a glance

Full reasoning on the Executive Summary and Risk Analysis pages.

✅ Recommended base case — SELL BEFORE EXPIRY (target 2027–2029), via IRS §1031 exchange

The NNN lease expires in 2030–2035 with no contractual renewal right (2026 FDD Item 17: 20-year term, rewrite at franchisor's sole discretion). With ~4–9 years of term left, the asset already prices in the 6.87–7.55% franchisee-QSR cap band; every year held moves it toward the cliff. Sell while term remains, defer the gain under §1031 (45-day ID / 180-day close; FTB Form 593 avoids CA 3⅓% withholding), and redeploy into lower-hazard, faster-growing replacement assets. Flip to HOLD only if a ≥10-year extension at/above current rent is signed now with a creditworthy tenant or corporate guarantee. Hard decision deadline: ≥24 months before exact expiry (≈ end-2031 using the 2033 midpoint).

⚠️ Key risks (all options analyzed on the Risk page)

Lease non-renewal at expiry · tenant (franchisee) default — JACK system shrinking (2,136 → guided 2,050–2,100 by end-2026) · cap-rate expansion with fed funds 3.75–4.00% and 10Y at 4.90% · black swans: earthquake (USGS 60% M6.7+/30yr; FEMA NRI 100.0 — highest in US), wildfire (Jan-2025 Eaton/Palisades: 16,256 structures destroyed), flood · CA insurance-market stress (FAIR Plan +8% YoY) · §1031 deadline execution risk · Prop 13 reassessment on sale.
