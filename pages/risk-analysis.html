Recommendation — balanced, risk-managed

Option A: sell before expiry (2027–2029 window), §1031 exchange into lower-hazard, higher-growth replacement assets. Flips to Option D (hold) only if a ≥10-year extension at/above current rent with escalations is signed now by a creditworthy tenant (ideally corporate-guaranteed). Hard decision deadline: ≥24 months before exact expiry (≈ end-2031 on the 2033 midpoint). Residual risk of path A is manageable: process risks (1031 deadlines, replacement selection) are controllable; black-swan casualty risk is carried via insurance + reserves + geographic diversification.
