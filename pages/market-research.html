Reading for the property decision

Strong, dense demand base ($81.4B city retail sales, 4.4–5.0% unemployment, +8.7% projected job growth) supports the QSR site and current buyer demand — but state population projections decline through the 2030–2035 window, and the county carries the nation's highest FEMA all-hazard risk with a stressed insurance market. Under NNN the tenant pays taxes/insurance during the term; the owner keeps casualty and post-expiry risk.
