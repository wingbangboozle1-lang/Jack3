Executive Summary

Prepared 2026-11-06 · Session 93f3087c · Subject: single-tenant Jack in the Box QSR, central Los Angeles, CA, NNN lease expiring 2030–2035.

The decision

Sell the property before the lease expires, or renew/hold through the 2030–2035 expiry based on the area's economic activity?

Recommendation (base case): SELL BEFORE EXPIRY — target a 2027–2029 sale window — via an IRS §1031 like-kind exchange

Rationale: (1) the lease has no contractual renewal right (2026 FDD Item 17), so expiry 2030–2035 is a hard income cliff; (2) with ~4–9 years of term remaining the asset already prices in the 6.87–7.55% franchisee-QSR cap band, and every year held erodes remaining term and price; (3) rates are high (fed funds 3.75–4.00%, 10Y 4.90%) with cap-rate expansion risk; (4) the tenant system is shrinking and 93% franchised, so the rent payer is a franchisee, not the corporation; (5) §1031 defers the gain (45/180-day rules; FTB Form 593 avoids CA 3⅓% withholding). Hold only if a ≥10-year extension at/above current rent is signed now with a creditworthy tenant or corporate guarantee. Hard decision deadline: ≥24 months before exact expiry (≈ end-2031 using the 2033 midpoint; exact date blank — anonymized).

Risk balance of the recommended path

  • Removed/reduced: expiry cliff, tenant-default-at-expiry, cap-rate time-decay — converted into a planned, tax-deferred exit.
  • Retained and managed: §1031 execution risk (engage a qualified intermediary before listing); replacement-asset selection (diversify, lower FEMA NRI / FHSZ); casualty black swans on retained assets (CEA earthquake cover, property/liability/loss-of-rent, reserves); recession (liquidity buffer, no leverage).
  • Overall residual risk: manageable and materially lower than holding to a 2030–2035 expiry with no renewal right.

Next steps

  • Obtain the lease internally (exact expiry, rent schedule, escalations, options, guarantees) — internal use only, never published.
  • Engage a qualified intermediary; pre-identify 2–3 replacement properties in lower-hazard, higher-growth markets.
  • List 2027–2029 (or immediately if a buyer offers ≥ NOI ÷ 6.5%); require §1031 mechanics + FTB Form 593.
  • If a ≥10-yr extension at acceptable rent is offered first, re-run the analysis — holding then becomes viable.
  • Until closing: keep CEA + property/liability/loss-of-rent cover; verify tenant insurance covenants annually.
