Decision — conditional, not categorical

The defensible output is a decision process and explicit evidence gaps, not a fabricated property-specific prediction.

Do not rely on the inherited categorical sale ranking. Evaluate an enforceable early extension and a real sale process in parallel.

The public evidence establishes regional context and issuer-system trends, but not Asset A's lease, guaranty, rent, tax basis, debt, operating status, alternative uses, insurance, or market value. Independent model tests also show that the inherited incumbent ranking is sensitive to asymmetric risk treatment.

Sell or qualifying exchange

Prefer only when verified net after-tax proceeds, debt payoff, transaction friction and replacement-asset risk compare favourably with the best enforceable hold/renewal alternative. Section 1031 is a possible mechanism for qualifying property, not an automatic benefit.

Official context: IRS §1031 overview ↗ · IRS Form 8824 instructions ↗

Renew / extend early

Prefer only when a signed term, actual rent payer and guaranty, net rent after concessions, expense obligations, exit rights and downside reserves produce a better risk-adjusted after-tax outcome than verified net sale proceeds. Do not use the inherited S3 downtime/capex case as a clean early-renewal proxy.

Official context: SEC FY2025 10-K ↗ · Issuer closure release ↗

Extend, then sell

Use only if an enforceable extension creates bid improvement that exceeds rent concessions and transaction costs. Compare actual bids; the inherited cap-compression assumption is not evidence of uplift.

Official context: SEC FY2025 10-K ↗

Wealth-preservation default

Until private diligence is complete, avoid irreversible capital commitments and preserve both options. Obtain the lease and guaranty, store-level rent coverage, insurance terms, title/zoning review, independent valuation and after-tax sale/hold comparisons privately. Do not publish those records.

Evidence type: analyst interpretation / model review; no official site-specific observation claimed.

Material source & narrative findings

These findings are independent observations from the reviewed sources and inherited narratives. “Critical” means capable of changing the decision, not that the subject tenant will default or close.

S-01 · critical

Del Taco metrics were attributed to the Jack in the Box analysis

The FY2025 10-K's Jack in the Box franchise table reports AUV of $1,874 thousand versus $1,949 thousand and royalties of 5.1% versus 5.0%. The inherited $1,549/$1,573 and 4.6%/4.7% values occur in the separate Del Taco table. This is a material brand-mapping error in rows marked VERIFIED.

Official context: SEC FY2025 10-K ↗

S-02 · critical

Franchise termination is not real-estate lease expiry

The issuer's closure announcement uses respective franchise agreement termination dates. It does not identify Asset A, establish the same date in its real-estate lease, or prove closure. Separately, the 10-K says the issuer has generally been able to renew restaurant leases at then-current market rates. Both facts belong in a balanced decision.

Official context: Issuer closure release ↗ · SEC FY2025 10-K ↗

S-03 · high

The linked BLS summary has rolled forward

The November 4 release reports September payrolls of 6,282.1 thousand / +0.5%, local and U.S. all-items CPI of +3.7% each, energy +20.3% local / +20.4% U.S., and September ECI wages/salaries +3.9% local. Earlier August/June values must be labelled as historical snapshots rather than current observations.

Official context: BLS LA summary ↗

S-04 · high

R-05 compares different model horizons

The inherited 12.30 R base value is S4 at 2035, while 2.51 R pessimistic is S4 at 2030. Same-horizon comparisons are 10.6433 to 2.5074 R for 2030 and 12.3042 to 4.8219 R for 2035. The reproducible model review isolates this mismatch.

S-06 · medium

Labour-force counts do not establish migration or causation

BLS shows both the metro labour force and unemployment count falling while the rate declined. It does not show why participation changed, establish out-migration, or prove that people leaving the labour force caused the improvement. Publish the components without the unsupported causal story.

Official context: BLS metro table ↗

S-07 · medium

No single wage rate can be called binding for Asset A

State general, city general and covered fast-food schedules have different dates and coverage rules. City jurisdiction, fast-food criteria, exemptions and any later industry adjustment are not established from the anonymous scope. The verified rates are context, not a site-specific payroll finding.

The county fact sheet reports 6,837 destroyed and 973 damaged; CAL FIRE's incident page reports 6,845 and 975. Preserve each source's values and date. Neither incident count establishes Asset A's wildfire exposure.

M-01 · confirmed
Inherited arithmetic reproduces

S1 is 15.28 R in base and 11.62 R in pessimistic; S3 2030 is 1.18 R in pessimistic. Reproduction is not appraisal validation.

M-02 · issue
R-05 mixes different expiry cases

The inherited 12.30 R to 2.51 R comparison pairs S4 base 2035 with S4 pessimistic 2030. Same-case pairs are 10.6433 to 2.5074 R (2030) and 12.3042 to 4.8219 R (2035).

M-03 · issue
Tenant-risk treatment is asymmetric

Only the three S3 results receive p_tenant_survives. The multiplier covers the whole PV, including pre-expiry rent also received by S2/S4/S6 and the terminal value.

M-04 · issue
The incumbent ranking is sensitive to that treatment

Removing only S3's extra tenant-survival multiplier changes S3 2035 ranks from 3/12/12 to 1/4/9 across optimistic/base/pessimistic. This isolates model dependence; it is not a corrected valuation.

M-05 · context
The decision criterion changes the winner

The inherited expected-value leader is S4 - Hold to expiry, re-let to another operator (+9y) at 2035 (17.15 R), while immediate sale leads only the selected pessimistic/maximin comparison.

M-06 · issue
Immediate sale is not preferred under base assumptions

Within the declared today-cap range and all other inputs at base, S5 extend-then-sell exceeds S1 at 5%, 6.25% and 8%. Immediate sale leads the selected pessimistic/maximin comparison, not every decision criterion inside the model.

M-07 · issue
S3 is not an early-renewal comparator

strategy_renew charges re-let downtime and capital at expiry. It may describe expiry/re-letting risk, but it is not a clean signed early extension with uninterrupted rent.

M-08 · limitation
This is not an after-tax wealth model

Immediate sale returns cap-rated proceeds less assumed friction. The code has no owner tax basis, debt payoff, income-tax calculation, replacement-asset return, or reinvestment-risk stream. R multiples cannot be presented as personal wealth outcomes.

M-09 · limitation
Probability inputs are assumptions

Tenant non-renewal, catastrophe probability and loss share, scenario weights, cap rates, downtime, capital costs and alternative-use value are declared assumptions. Regional hazard data do not calibrate parcel damage or closure probabilities.

Review method

  1. Read official pages, filings and the DOF workbook directly; record document period, geography, vintage and access date for each value.
  2. Use issuer-primary SEC filings and investor releases for tenant observations; never equate system metrics with Asset A performance.
  3. Keep observations, official projections, analyst interpretation and model-derived sensitivity outputs visibly separate.
  4. Leave unconfirmed property values blank. A blank is an evidence result, not zero, not not applicable, and not permission to infer.
  5. Run the inherited projection read-only, reproduce its arithmetic, then test strategy semantics and one-factor sensitivities. A passing audit test does not validate an appraisal.
  6. Generate this page from JSON and model results; verify source links on every table row, all internal anchors, deterministic output and prohibited personal/property identifiers.
  7. No trackers, cookies, external scripts, web fonts, maps, forms, address lookup or personal-information collection.
