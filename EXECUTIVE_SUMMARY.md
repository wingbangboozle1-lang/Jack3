### C. Statutory Transfer-Tax Cliffs, Prop 13 & IRC §1031 (`model/area_projection.py`)
- **Measure ULA dead zones (City of LA, effective July 1, 2026):** Base City (`0.45%`) + County (`0.11%`) = **`0.56%`** up to `$5,400,000`. Above `$5,400,000`, **4.0% Measure ULA** (`4.56%` total) applies to the **entire gross price**—creating a **$226,500 dead zone** (`$5,400,000` nets **`$5,369,760`** before brokerage vs. `$5,400,500` netting **`$5,154,237`**; recovery requires **`$5,626,500`**). At `$10,900,000`, the **`5.5%` tier** (`6.06%` total) creates a second dead zone from **`$10,900,000` (`$10,239,460` net)** to **`$11,074,000`**.
- **Proposition 13 `35-year` leasehold trap (`CA BOE Annotation 220.0345`):** Under R&TC §§ 61(c)(1) & 62(g), creating, extending, or transferring a lease where **remaining term plus renewal options ≥ 35 years** triggers a full **100% property-tax reassessment** (conversely, selling the fee while `<35` years remain triggers reassessment, whereas selling the fee under an existing `≥35`-year leasehold does not).
- **§1031 exchange vs. unshielded taxable sale (`IRS` & `CA FTB`):** IRC §1031 defers federal and state recognition on qualifying real property within strict 45-day identification / 180-day closing clocks; an unshielded cash sale realizes federal LTCG (`0%/15%/20%`) + up to `25%` unrecaptured §1250 depreciation + NIIT + **California taxing all capital gains as ordinary income**.
