13A / PRIVATE CALCULATOR
Private tool — the boundary in one line. Everything you type stays in this browser tab: this page makes no network requests, stores nothing, sets no cookies and loads no external asset. How this tool respects the privacy boundary

Same boundary as the rest of the site
  • No network access, no storage, no cookies, no analytics, no forms — enforced at build time by the verification standard and its automated gates.
  • Every constant on this page is a verified rule with an official link; every personal field is blank in the published register and stays blank unless you type it here.
  • Asset A and Owner A remain placeholders; this page does not know and does not ask for the address, the parcel number, the rent, the basis or the offers.
  • Print results for advisers; close the tab and nothing persists.
  • The calculator computes; it does not decide. The decision-gate progress board lists what must be verified before any route is chosen.
Independent decision-support research, not an appraisal, legal opinion or personalised financial advice.
