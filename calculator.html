What this worksheet does not do

Deliberate limits; a CPA runs the authoritative version (SG-01 option C).
  • It models neither income tax on rents during the hold, nor depreciation going forward, nor cost segregation.
  • It does not model §1031 deferral, installment sales, opportunity zones, or an entity-level sale.
  • NIIT is applied to the whole gain when selected; the statutory lesser-of MAGI test is not modelled.
  • California taxes the whole gain as ordinary income; no separate state recapture is computed.
  • The hold case assumes the same adjusted basis at exit and no capital expenditure during the hold.
  • Every rate and price is owner-supplied except the verified public rules table above. This page is arithmetic, not tax advice.
