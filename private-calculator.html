Private browser calculator

An arithmetic-only comparison of a documented sale-now net amount with hold and negotiated-renewal cases. No property or owner values are prefilled; no option is selected.

Use only dated, documented inputs. Sale-now line items should come from a written offer, escrow and the owner’s CPA/tax counsel. The hold and renewal cash flows and exit proceeds are scenarios; label any assumption as unverified in your private workpapers. A positive difference means only that the entered scenario has a larger calculated present value than the sale-now net under the selected discount rate. It is not a recommendation, appraisal, tax calculation or risk ranking.

The arithmetic does not choose a winner. It assumes a level after-tax cash flow received at each year-end, a terminal net amount at the end of the selected horizon, no growth, and the same user-selected annual discount rate for both hold cases. It does not model probabilities, changing tax rules, lease-option timing, vacancy, inflation, risk tolerance or reinvestment opportunities unless reflected in the inputs.

Formula and decision limits

Arithmetic only; tax and market inputs remain external

Present-value formula

For each hold case: PV = annual net cash flow × annuity factor + terminal net proceeds ÷ (1 + r)N. The annuity factor is (1 − (1 + r)−N) ÷ r; when r = 0, it equals N. All annual flows are assumed to arrive at year-end.

The sale-now net amount is not discounted because it is measured today. Keep all amounts in the same currency and use a consistent nominal or real-dollar convention with the matching discount rate.

What this tool does not calculate

It does not compute taxable gain, adjusted basis, depreciation recapture, capital-gain brackets, NIIT, California tax, Measure ULA, county transfer tax or §1031 eligibility. It only subtracts the tax and cost amounts entered by the user. Tax and legal rules can change before a 2030–2035 transaction; obtain a current professional calculation.

A higher modeled present value is not a personalized wealth recommendation. The sale, hold and renewal alternatives also differ in liquidity, concentration, catastrophe exposure and feasibility.
