## 🔍 Risk Analysis

### Decision Path Risks

| Path | Risk Level | Primary Risks | Mitigation |
|------|------------|---------------|------------|
| **Sell with §1031** | Medium | Replacement property risk, price risk, timing risk | Start early, identify multiple replacements, pre-approve financing |
| **Extend, then sell/hold** | Medium | Tenant may not offer acceptable terms, rent may be below market | Demand ≥10 years at market rent with strong credit support |
| **Hold to expiry** | **HIGH** | Vacancy at expiry, tenant default, capital calls | **REJECT** - Too risky for wealth preservation |
| **Re-tenant/reposition** | Medium | Downtime, incentives, entitlement, construction cost | Fallback only with verified demand |
| **Redevelop** | High | Permitting, environmental, financing | Not wealth-preservation route |
| **Sale without exchange** | Medium | Immediate tax liability | Rational only if liquidity dominates |

### Black Swan Events

| Event | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|-------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Wildfire** | Medium-High | Total loss, business interruption, insurance withdrawal | Verify hazard zone, ensure coverage, maintain defensible space |
| **Earthquake** | High | Structural damage, business interruption, high deductibles | Obtain earthquake insurance, verify seismic retrofitting |
| **Flood** | Low-Medium | Water damage, business interruption | Verify FEMA zone, obtain flood insurance |
| **Economic Downturn** | Medium | Lower values, higher cap rates, longer marketing | Maintain strong tenant credit, size reserves |
| **Tenant Bankruptcy** | Medium | Lease rejection, vacancy, re-letting costs | Obtain corporate guaranty, monitor performance |
