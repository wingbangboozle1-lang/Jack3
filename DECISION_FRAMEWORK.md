## Risk Assessment by Decision Path

### Sell with §1031 Replacement (PRIMARY TRACK)

**Risks:**
- ⚠️ **Replacement property risk** - May not find suitable replacement within 45/180 days
- ⚠️ **Price risk** - Market may decline during marketing period
- ⚠️ **Timing risk** - May need to close before replacement is identified
- ⚠️ **Financing risk** - Replacement may require new financing
- ✅ **Tax deferral** - Major benefit if replacement found

**Mitigations:**
- Start marketing early (24-36 months before expiry)
- Work with qualified intermediary (QI) early
- Identify multiple potential replacement properties
- Obtain financing pre-approval for replacements
- Consider DST (Delaware Statutory Trust) as backup

**Best For:** Owners who want to preserve capital, defer taxes, and maintain real estate exposure

---

### Extend, Then Sell or Hold (PARALLEL TRACK)

**Risks:**
- ⚠️ **Tenant may not offer acceptable terms** - May demand concessions or short term
- ⚠️ **Rent may be below market** - Current lease may not reflect current market rates
- ⚠️ **Credit may deteriorate** - Tenant financial condition may weaken
- ⚠️ **Property may become obsolete** - Building may need updates to remain competitive
- ⚠️ **Opportunity cost** - May miss better investment opportunities

**Mitigations:**
- Demand ≥10 year term with market rent
- Require strong credit support (corporate guaranty preferred)
- Include rent escalations tied to CPI or fixed %
- Require tenant to maintain property in good condition
- Include early termination option for owner

**Best For:** Owners who have strong tenant relationships and want to maintain income stream

---

### Hold to Expiry Unchanged (REJECTED)

**Risks:**
- ❌ **Vacancy risk at expiry** - 100% income loss if tenant doesn't renew
- ❌ **Tenant default risk** - May default before expiry
- ❌ **Capital calls risk** - May need to fund major repairs at worst time
- ❌ **Opportunity cost** - May miss better investment opportunities
- ❌ **Inflation risk** - Fixed rent may not keep up with costs

**Mitigations:**
- Size reserves to cover 12-24 months of vacancy
- Obtain strong tenant credit
- Maintain property in excellent condition
- Monitor tenant performance closely

**Verdict:** **REJECT** - Too much risk for wealth preservation objective

## Black Swan Event Analysis

### Wildfire (Probability: Medium-High in LA County)

**2025 Precedent:** Palisades and Eaton fires destroyed **16,264 structures** in 24 days  
**Impact:**
- Total loss of building (if in fire path)
- Business interruption (even if building survives)
- Insurance may be unavailable or very expensive post-event
- Regional economic impact (lower property values, higher costs)

**Mitigations:**
- Verify fire hazard severity zone
- Ensure adequate insurance coverage (including business interruption)
- Consider wildfire-resistant building materials
- Maintain defensible space
- Have evacuation plan

**Decision Impact:** If in high wildfire risk zone, **accelerate sale timeline** or demand higher return

---

### Earthquake (Probability: High in Southern California)

**USGS Model:** M7.8 southern San Andreas = ~1,800 deaths, ~$213bn losses  
**Impact:**
- Structural damage or total loss
- Business interruption (months to years)
- Insurance deductibles may be high (10-20% of replacement cost)
- Earthquake insurance may be separate policy
- Regional economic disruption

**Mitigations:**
- Obtain earthquake insurance (separate from standard policy)
- Verify building meets current seismic codes
- Consider seismic retrofitting
- Maintain emergency preparedness plan

**Decision Impact:** If building is not seismically retrofitted, **factor in higher risk premium**

---

### Flood (Probability: Low-Medium in Central LA)

**Impact:**
- Water damage to building and equipment
- Business interruption
- Mold and other secondary damage
- Insurance may have flood exclusions

**Mitigations:**
- Verify FEMA flood zone
- Obtain flood insurance if in flood zone
- Ensure proper drainage around property
- Maintain flood barriers if applicable

**Decision Impact:** If in flood zone, **verify insurance coverage and factor into risk assessment**

---

### Economic Downturn (Probability: Medium)

**Impact:**
- Lower property values
- Higher cap rates (lower prices for same NOI)
- Tenant may struggle (QSR is somewhat recession-resistant)
- Financing may be harder to obtain
- Longer marketing period for sale

**Mitigations:**
- Maintain strong tenant credit
- Keep debt levels manageable
- Size reserves appropriately
- Diversify across multiple properties/tenants

**Decision Impact:** If economic indicators are weakening, **accelerate sale timeline**

---

### Tenant Bankruptcy (Probability: Medium for QSR Industry)

**Jack in the Box Context:**
- Corporate has been closing underperforming stores (150-200 targeted)
- System same-store sales: -1.1% (Q3 FY2026)
- Franchise margin: 37.4% (down from 39.3%)
- Selling owned real estate to pay down debt

**Impact:**
- If corporate tenant: Potential rejection of lease in bankruptcy
- If franchisee tenant: Higher risk of default
- Vacancy and re-letting costs
- Potential need to negotiate with new tenant

**Mitigations:**
- Obtain corporate guaranty if tenant is franchisee
- Monitor tenant financial performance
- Require strong security deposit
- Maintain good relationship with franchisor

**Decision Impact:** If tenant is franchisee with weak credit, **demand corporate guaranty or consider sale**
