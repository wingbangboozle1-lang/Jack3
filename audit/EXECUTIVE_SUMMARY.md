## Decision risks (analyst interpretation)

Selling removes future rent and site exposure but introduces tax, execution and reinvestment risk. Holding retains income and concentration while expiry approaches. Renewing requires acceptable rent, concessions, credit and enforceable expense obligations; it does not guarantee increased value. Renew-then-sell depends on actual bid improvement net of concessions. Re-tenanting or redevelopment adds vacancy, construction, financing and permitting exposure. A qualifying Section 1031 exchange may defer recognition; it is not automatically suitable or tax elimination. Official IRS context: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-estate-tax-tips
