Projections 2026 to 2035

This page is arithmetic, not prophecy. It extends published official trends forward and shows every step. It assigns no probability to any scenario, because no official source assigns one.

What this is not

No government agency publishes a forecast for this parcel, and no official source ranks the scenarios below. The tables are extensions of measured aggregate trends for Los Angeles, offered so that the direction and the magnitude of the arithmetic are visible. A single restaurant site can move against its region in either direction.

How to use these grids

  • Choose the column matching the actual remaining term once you have the expiry date.
  • Choose the row matching the discount rate you believe a buyer would use: the verified 4.84% risk-free anchor plus your judgement of the spread.
  • Multiply the cell by your annual base rent. The result is the present value of the contracted rent alone, before any residual value, tax, closing cost or vacancy assumption.
  • Compare that figure with what the market would pay. That comparison needs a cap rate, which no official source publishes — see the flagged items.

Formula: PV = sum over t = 0 to n-1 of ((1 + g) / (1 + d)) ^ t, with g = 0 for the first grid and g = 3.7% for the second. Published to two decimal places; the build computes it deterministically.
