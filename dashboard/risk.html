On black swan events

Wildfire and earthquake are not symmetrical with ordinary business risk. A 60% thirty-year likelihood of a magnitude 6.7 or greater earthquake in the Los Angeles region is a published USGS figure, but it says nothing about this building, its construction, its retrofit history or its soil. Those are engineering questions, and they need a site-specific answer before any of the pathway comparisons are meaningful.
