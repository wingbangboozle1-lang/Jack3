Sources and method

21 official sources, every one fetched and read during this session. No aggregator, brokerage, law firm or news site was used as a source of any published value.

The rules this layer follows

  • Official or blank. A value is published only if it was read from the linked official source during this session. Otherwise the cell is left empty and flagged.
  • Derived means the arithmetic is shown. A derived cell prints its inputs and its formula on the page. It is never presented as an observation.
  • No inherited trust. Nothing was copied from an earlier layer of this repository. Where an earlier session's figure was useful but not re-verified, it is on the flagged page instead.
  • No probabilities. Where no agency publishes a likelihood, no likelihood is asserted.
  • No personal information. No name, address, parcel identifier, email, phone number or account handle is collected or published. The asset is called Asset A throughout.
  • No scripts, no trackers, no forms, no third-party assets. The site is static HTML and one stylesheet.

Limitations

  • Regional data is not a parcel forecast. Census, BLS and CDE figures describe a city or a county, not a site.
  • Issuer filings describe a system of 2,115 restaurants, not the one you own.
  • Source links were read on the snapshot date. Published series are revised; the BLS area summary is a rolling document and its contents change with each release.
  • Cap rates, comparable sales, insurance premiums and construction costs have no official source and are therefore absent.
  • Severity ratings in the risk register are analyst judgement applied to cited evidence, not official ratings.
  • The projection tables extend published trends arithmetically. They are not forecasts and carry no probability weight.
  • This is not an appraisal, a legal opinion, or personalised financial, tax or investment advice.
