The four blockers

K-F-01 through K-F-04 are the ones that stop the analysis. Without the deed, the lease, the rent and the obligor, no price, no cash-flow forecast and no ranking can be verified for this property. Every other item on this page refines an answer; those four decide whether an answer exists.
