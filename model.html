Known limits

Read before relying on anything here
  • ACS estimates carry sampling error; no margins of error were obtained.
  • QuickFacts mixes vintages and survey periods; different vintages are not comparable.
  • The BLS area summary is not seasonally adjusted and may be revised.
  • Issuer results describe a chain, not this restaurant or its legal obligor.
  • Hazard figures are scenarios and regional forecasts, not parcel exposure.
  • IRS Topic 409 displays 2025 brackets while Rev. Proc. 2025-32 establishes finalized 2026 brackets; draft 2027 Rev. Proc. 2026-35 carries placeholder dates and is withheld.
  • Rolling sources change: re-read the official pages on every release.
