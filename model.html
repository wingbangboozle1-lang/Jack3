10 / THE PROJECTION SYSTEM

Projection framework

The reproducible half of a forecast: verified anchors, stated arithmetic and a stress grid. The half that cannot be honestly supplied is named rather than filled with a number.

The honest part of a forecast

Every verified trend in this file is flat, shrinking, or rising in cost — and none of them is a forecast for this parcel.

A projection that survives scrutiny has three parts: verified anchors, arithmetic that anyone can reproduce, and an explicit statement of what is assumed. This page publishes the first two in full and names the third rather than filling it in. No 2030–2035 sale price, cap rate, rent or renewal probability appears anywhere on this site, because no official source publishes one for this property and compounding a regional trend into a price would be an artefact of the rule, not evidence.

Risk-free only

$0.826

At the 4.88% 10-year Treasury yield of 9 November 2026, four years out: 1 ÷ 1.0488⁴. This is a floor for a discount rate, not a recommended rate. A property discount rate adds a premium for illiquidity, single-tenant concentration and stale rent, and that premium must be declared as an assumption rather than borrowed from a source that does not publish it.

What this model refuses to do

  • Produce a 2030–2035 value. No official source publishes cap rates, and none is asserted.
  • Assign a renewal probability. No agency publishes one for a private lease.
  • Extrapolate the 2020–2025 population decline, or the 2026 payroll growth, to 2035.
  • Quote a store-level sales figure. The tenant does not publish unit-level results.

How to use it

Four steps, in order
Anchor

Write down the 10-year yield and the local inflation rate on the day you decide. Both are published and both move.

Bound

Set an escalator floor at local CPI and a ceiling at what the tenant will sign. Anything outside that range needs a reason.

Stress

Run the exit at 6.0% and 8.0%. If the deal only works at the low cap, the price is the risk.

Gate

Do not convert a scenario into a decision until the lease, the estoppel and the owner's basis are in hand.

Independent decision-support research on public sources. Not an appraisal, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a personalised recommendation. Asset A and Owner A are placeholders. Every published value carries a link to the official page it came from; anything that could not be confirmed is blank and listed on Flagged for review.
