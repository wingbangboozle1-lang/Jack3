## 1. Executive Recommendation (Conditional Two-Step Playbook)

**Do not passively hold the lease into the final 24–36 months before expiration (`P4`), and do not execute an unshielded taxable cash sale (`P3`) unless personal liquidity requires it.** Instead, execute a **disciplined Two-Step Blend-or-1031 Playbook** calibrated to your exact lease verification gates:

1. **Step 1 — Immediate Document Audit & Early Extension Test (Q4 2026 – Q2 2027, Zero Capital at Risk):**
   - Confirm four private lease facts from your executed contract (`Flags F-02..F-07`): (a) whether **Jack in the Box Inc. (Corporate)** or a **franchisee entity** is on the lease/guaranty, (b) exact expiry year (`2030` vs. `2035`), (c) store-level gross sales if reported, and (d) total original lease duration vs. California's **35-year Proposition 13 leasehold reassessment threshold** ([CA BOE R&TC §§ 61(c), 62(g)](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/220_0345.pdf)).
   - Approach the tenant early (after Jack in the Box's FY2026 Form 10-K release on **December 9, 2026**, [Issuer IR](https://investors.jackinthebox.com/news/news-details/2026/Jack-in-the-Box-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Earnings-Webcast/default.aspx)) to test a **10-to-15-year blend-and-extend** (keeping total remaining term + renewal options **strictly `< 35 years`** so no Prop 13 reassessment is triggered).
2. **Step 2A — If Tenant Commits to a ≥10-Year Corporate/Strong Extension (`Pathway P1`):**
   - Execute the extension to restore long-term NNN financeability, **then sell the freshly extended NNN asset in 2027–2028 via an IRC §1031 Like-Kind Exchange** ([IRS §1031](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-estate-tax-tips)) into 2–3 diversified replacement properties. In our symmetric R-normalized model (`1.00 R` = 1 year of base rent), `P1` delivers **`14.50 R` expected PV** and the **highest downside/black-swan floor (`13.02 R` Downside / `11.92 R` Tail Stress)** across both 2030 and 2035 expiries. Hold long-term *only* if corporate-guaranteed, store rent-to-sales is healthy (`≤8%–9%`), and this parcel is a small slice of your net worth.
3. **Step 2B — If Tenant Declines Early Extension, Seeks a Steep Rent Cut, or Lease Is Franchisee-Only (`Pathway P2`):**
   - **Sell before lease expiry via an IRC §1031 Exchange while financeable term remains** — **immediately in 2027** if expiry is near `2030–2031` (`4–5` years left), or **in 2027–2028** if expiry is `2033–2035` (`6–8` years left, before crossing the ~5-year commercial mortgage financing cliff).
   - **Price-Cliff Discipline:** If the parcel is inside the City of Los Angeles, effective **July 1, 2026** Measure ULA imposes a **4.0% surtax on the entire gross price above `$5,400,000`** (`4.56%` combined City+County transfer tax vs. `0.56%` at/below `$5,400,000`, [LA Office of Finance](https://finance.lacity.gov/faq/measure-ula)). Any gross price between `$5,400,001` and `~$5,626,000` nets the seller **less cash** than closing at `$5,400,000`!
