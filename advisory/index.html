Executive Decision Verdict: Conditional Two-Step Blend-or-1031 Playbook

Do not drift passively into the final 24–36 months of the 2030–2035 lease term, and avoid a taxable cash sale without an IRC §1031 exchange. Verified official evidence shows a flat-to-shrinking regional population (LA City −0.8%, LA County −3.2% since April 2020), modest metro job growth (+0.5% YoY Sept 2026), statutory $20.00/hr QSR wage pressure + +20.3% energy CPI, elevated risk-free yields (4.86% 10-Yr U.S. Treasury on Nov 6, 2026), and an actively contracting tenant system (2,191 → 2,115 units; Q3 FY26 comps −1.1%; $500M June 2026 refinancing at 7.624%).

  • Step 1 (Q4 2026 – Q2 2027 — Zero-Cost Audit & Early Extension Test): Verify lease obligor (Corporate vs. Franchisee — 10-K Item 2 shows 1,585 corporate-leased vs. 387 franchisee-direct units), exact expiry year, unit gross sales, and Prop 13 35-year leasehold status. After Jack in the Box's FY2026 10-K on Dec 9, 2026, test an early 10–15 yr blend-and-extend (capping total remaining term + options strictly < 35 years per CA BOE R&TC §61(c)).
  • Step 2A (If Tenant Signs ≥10-Yr Extension → Pathway P1): Sell the freshly extended NNN lease in 2027–2028 via an IRC §1031 exchange (14.50 R expected PV; 11.92 R black-swan floor) to capture long-term cap-rate pricing while diversifying out of single-brand and LA seismic/wildfire/insurance tail risk.
  • Step 2B (If Tenant Declines Extension or Lease Is Franchisee-Only → Pathway P2): Sell before lease expiry via IRC §1031 exchange in 2027–2028 while financeable lease term remains, pricing with strict discipline around the City of LA's $5,400,000 Measure ULA 4.0% cliff (effective July 1, 2026).
