Risk Architecture: 12 Business-as-Usual Risks & 8 Rare Black-Swan Shocks

During the remaining NNN lease term, routine property tax, insurance, and maintenance increases pass through to the tenant — until occupancy costs break unit-level profitability at renewal, or a major casualty triggers a lease termination clause. Furthermore, Los Angeles County experienced a realized black-swan event in January 2025 (Palisades & Eaton Fires: 16,251 structures destroyed and $5.2B–$10.1B county economic output lost), prompting a temporary commercial FAIR Plan expansion that sunsets December 31, 2028 — directly ahead of the 2030–2035 lease expiration window.
