Methodology Disclosure: Transparent R-Normalized Projection System

Because the subject property's street address, contractual rent, exact expiry year within 2030–2035, and tax basis are private/unsupplied, no parcel dollar figures are invented. Instead, our projection engine (advisory_src/model.py) anchors regional trajectories to verified Census V2025, Sept 2026 BLS, and Nov 6, 2026 U.S. Treasury (4.86% 10-Yr CMT) data, and evaluates all 5 owner pathways symmetrically in multiples of 1.00 R (= 1 year of current contractual base rent). Multiply any R figure by your actual annual base rent to convert immediately to dollars.
