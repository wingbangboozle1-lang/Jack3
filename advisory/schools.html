Public School Systems (CDE / LAUSD) & Educational Attainment

Official California Department of Education 2025–26 enrollment, grade-level cohort structure, 2025 CA School Dashboard graduation performance, and Census attainment.

School Systems Takeaway: Graduating Performance Steady (86.5%), Youth Cohorts Shrinking

Los Angeles Unified remains California's largest school district at 497,105 students in 2025–26 with a maintained 86.5% combined 4/5-year graduation rate (2025 Dashboard). Crucially for long-term neighborhood demographics, incoming Kindergarten/Grade 1 shares (6.3%–6.7%) are smaller than high-school Grade 9–12 shares (7.5%–8.2%), corroborating continued family demographic headwinds through 2030–2035.
