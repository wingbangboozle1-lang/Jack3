Central Los Angeles Geography, Zoning & Transit Catalysts

Evaluating urban density, DTLA 2040 upzoning capacity, the newly opened May 2026 Metro D Line Subway Extension, and parcel-level entitlement gates.

Geography Takeaway: High Urban Land Floor, But Drive-Thru Rights Are Parcel-Specific

Los Angeles City packs 8,304.2 residents/sq mi across 469.49 sq mi, and two major public investments — Metro's D Line Subway Extension Section 1 (opened May 8, 2026) and the DTLA 2040 Community Plan (100,000 new homes & 100,000 jobs) — strengthen Central LA infill land residual values. However, parcel zoning, TOC tier, and whether a vacant drive-thru loses nonconforming rights cannot be verified without the withheld APN (Flags F-01, F-09, F-10).
