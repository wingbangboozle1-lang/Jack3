04 / Population, Income & Housing

Demographics, Household Income, Housing Costs & Retail Volume

Line-by-line U.S. Census QuickFacts (Vintage 2025 & 2020–2024 ACS) and official CA Dept. of Finance vs. SCAG long-range demographic forecasts.

Demographics Takeaway: Large High-Value Urban Base Facing Mild Out-Migration & Rent Squeeze

Measured U.S. Census Vintage 2025 estimates confirm population contraction from April 2020 to July 2025 in both LA City (−0.8% to 3,869,089) and LA County (−3.2% to 9,694,934), aligning with CA DOF/EWDD projections of decline (~9.3M by 2040) rather than older SCAG growth forecasts. High median home values ($921,200 City) support land value, while 16.5% City poverty and 64.0% renter share heighten QSR menu-price sensitivity.
